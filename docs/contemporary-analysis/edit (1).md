## **Soil Solidarity**

A Register Nevada Non-profit info@soilsolidarity.org Katie 775-842-2545

Subject: **Collaborative Support for Housing Solutions**

Community Faith Leaders,

Hope this letter finds you in good health and spirits. As leaders who play a pivotal role in guiding our communities towards compassion, justice, and the common good, we are writing to you today to discuss a matter of utmost importance – housing solutions for our fellow neighbors in need.

We are all witnesses to the growing challenges faced by individuals and families struggling to secure stable and affordable housing. The lack of affordable housing has far-reaching consequences, affecting not only an individual's well-being but also the social fabric of our communities. Families that are burdened by housing costs often face difficult choices between paying for housing, healthcare, education, and other basic necessities.

As faith leaders, you have a unique and powerful platform to create positive change and advocate for social justice. By uniting our efforts, we can make a significant impact in addressing the housing crisis that our communities are facing. It is a shared responsibility to ensure that everyone has access to safe and affordable housing, as this aligns with the core principles of compassion, empathy, and dignity that are central to your faith.

There has been an 875% increase in homelessness since 2017. With more than 1800 people sleeping outside in Washoe County, our social, medical, and judicial institutions are stretched. In the August 2022 paper, "An In-depth Guide of Homelessness in Washoe County"\* it was strongly recommended that our community pursue small tailored housing programs, vs large warehouses for the poor. This is an achievable goal if we work together.

We urge you to consider the following steps as we collaborate to support comprehensive housing solutions:

**Housing Solutions:** Faith Leaders could shift into supporting small tailor housing programs. Identify core team members in your organization that can spearhead a plan to encourage congregations, identify sites, fundraise, and actively involve themselves in the operations.

**Raise Awareness:** Use your pulpit, congregational meetings, and other gatherings to educate your community about the importance of affordable housing and the challenges faced by vulnerable individuals and families.

**Advocacy:** Actively engage in advocacy efforts to encourage local, state, and national policymakers to prioritize and implement policies that promote affordable housing development, tenant protections, and supportive services.

**Partnerships:** Explore partnerships with other congregations, local nonprofit organizations, housing agencies, and community groups to collectively address the housing crisis. Your congregation can provide valuable resources such as land, facilities, or volunteer support.

**Supportive Services:** Work to ensure that housing solutions are not just about providing a roof over one's head but also encompass supportive services that address mental health, addiction, employment, and other factors that contribute to housing instability.

**Foster Dialogue:** Organize community dialogues and events to promote understanding and compassion, breaking down stigmas associated with homelessness and housing instability. Fundraising and Donations: Create fundraising initiatives within your congregation to support affordable housing projects or contribute to existing housing organizations.

Our combined efforts can create a lasting impact on the lives of many individuals and families who are currently facing housing insecurity. By coming together as faith leaders and communities, we can foster an environment where housing is recognized as a fundamental human right and a key component of social justice.

We are confident that your leadership, wisdom, and commitment to your faith's values will contribute greatly to the success of these efforts. Let us work hand in hand to build a society where everyone has a place to call home, and where the principles of compassion, empathy, and justice guide our actions.

This letter is not simply a plea for help. We want to work together! Collectively, we have a ton of experience with mutual aid, grassroots organizing, and local homelessness. Attached to this letter are details about Soil Solidarity's housing pilot program.\* We are actively pursuing these goals and would love your support. If you would like to schedule a meeting with us to discuss ways your faith community can participate in solving one of the most important needs in our community please email [info@soilsolidarity.org.](mailto:info@soilsolidarity.org)

Thank you for your attention to this critical matter. We look forward to the opportunity to collaborate and create positive change together.

Sincerely,

## Katie Colling Executive Director
