# HABITABILITY

- **Your landlord must maintain your rental property in a habitable condition, which generally means that it is livable so that an average person could reside there in reasonable comfort.**
- **You have these rights whether you have a written lease or not When they fail, there ARE steps you can take!**

**Habitability is also a legal defense to eviction that you can use when your home is not in a habitable/livable condition.**

## ![](_page_6_Picture_0.jpeg)

### IS YOUR PROBLEM "ESSENTIAL" OR "NONESSENTIAL"?

- **It is important to distinguish whether your problem is " essential" or " nonessential" because each has different timeframes and remedies.**
- **You can 't make a habitability defense if the problem was caused deliberately or negligently by you, a member of your household, or someone on the premises with your consent.**

# ESSENTIAL SERVICES

- **Essential items or services include:**
 - **Heat/Air Conditioning**
 - **Running/Hot Water**
 - **Electricity**
 - **Gas**
 - **Functioning Door Lock**
 - **Or another essential item or service, without which your rental property is not livable.**

# ESSENTIAL SERVICES: STEP 1

- **If you have a problem with your rental property, you must give written notice of the problem to your landlord or property manager. It should be clear on what you are asking the landlord or manager to fix.**
 - **Simply telling your landlord about the problem or submitting a work order is not enough.**
 - **Avoid any possibility of your notice being disputed.**

### APPLICATION FEES

- **Fees for applications, credit checks, and background checks MUST be refunded if:**
 - **The unit is rented to someone else AND**
 - **The background or credit check hasn 't been run.**
- Applications, backgrounds, or credit checks CANNOT be charged to a minor.

### CLEAR LEASE

- **Your rent must be listed as ONE AMOUNT!**
- **This includes ALL mandatory fees.**
- **Landlords CANNOT CHARGE MORE than that amount.**

### CLEAR LEASE

- **Utilities can be billed separately only IF:**
 - **The utility company cannot directly bill you or the water is master metered (metered for the whole building, not per unit).**
- **There MUST be a clear explanation of the reason above. The landlord cannot charge more than the actual cost of the water, gas, or electric for your unit.**

### RENT PAYMENTS

- **Landlords must provide at least one method to pay rent or fees that:**
 - **Does NOT include extra fees; and**
 - **Does NOT require your bank account info.**

### RENT PAYMENTS

- **IF you pay online:**
 - **The charge CANNOT be more than the online portal' s actual fee.**
 - **This fee MUST be clearly listed in your rental agreement.**

### ENFORCEMENT & PENALTIES

- If your landlord breaks these rules, you can sue them.
- A judge can award damages, attorney's fees, and up to \$250 for each deceptive violation.
