**Aposimz** (stylized in [all caps\)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_caps), known in Japan as **Ningyō no Kuni** (⼈形の国, lit.'Country of Dolls'), is a Japanese [manga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manga) series written and illustrated by [Tsutomu Nihei.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsutomu_Nihei) It was serialized in [Kodansha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kodansha)'s shō[nen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Dnen_manga) [manga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Dnen_manga) magazine [Monthly Sh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monthly_Sh%C5%8Dnen_Sirius)ōnen Sirius from February 2017 to August 2021, with its chapters collected in nine [tank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tank%C5%8Dbon)ōbon volumes.

The series takes place on an artificial celestial body known as Aposimz (アポシムズ, Aposhimuzu), which is 200,000 kilometers in diameter. Most of the planet's volume is occupied by its core, which is covered by a superstructural shell. Five thousand years ago, people who lost a war against the inhabitants of the core were exiled to the surface, a cold and harsh environment. Surface dwellers face both the rapidlyspreading Frame Disease (⼈形病, Ningyō-byō), which slowly converts normal humans into biomechanical, zombie-like beings, and aggressive native biomechanical creatures known as automatons. Certain people have been transformed by special "codes" into Regular Frames (正規⼈形, Seiki Ningyō), beings composed of a substance called placenta (胞⾐, ena) and fueled by Heigus Particles (ヘイグス粒⼦, Heigusu Ryūshi), which renders them biologically immortal and makes them stronger and more resilient than normal humans. Regular Frames are also able to assume an armored form and each one has a unique special ability or weapon. These Regular Frames are highly revered by normal humans and are often leaders and protectors in their communities.

# Etherow (エスロー, Esurō)

The sole survivor of the White Diamond Beam (⽩菱の梁, Hakubishi no Hari) settlement after the Rebedoa Empire (リベドア帝国, Ribedoa Teikoku) destroys it. After being transformed into a Regular Frame, he seeks revenge upon the Empire. His armored form is deep red in color and features a miniature [railgun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Railgun) embedded in the left forearm known as an EBTG, which allows him to fire a variety of ammunition from his body's own solidified placenta to the incredibly powerful Anti-Megastructure Bullets.

# Titania (タイターニア, Taitānia)

An intelligent automaton who provides Etherow with the code that transforms him into a Regular Frame as well as seven Anti-Megastructure Bullets (AMBs). She journeys with Etherow as his guide and confidant. Her normal form is that of a small four-legged creature, but she is able to temporarily assume a humanoid form. However, this transformation consumes a large amount of energy and she is only able to change once daily.

## Keisha (ケーシャ, Kēsha)

The princess of the underground settlement of Irf Nikk (イ ルフ・ニク, Irufu Niku) and a Regular Frame who eventually joins Etherow and Titania. Her armored form possesses a staff weapon that can extend to great lengths and is able to channel electricity.

# Wasabu (ワサブ)

A Regular Frame and former member of the Rebedoa Empire's Reincarnated (転⽣者, Tensei-sha) who joins Etherow's group. As a Regular Frame, Wasabu is capable of reinforcing himself in armor. He is able to fly and is able to kill anyone whose skin is exposed in the air.

Aposimz is written and illustrated by [Tsutomu Nihei.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsutomu_Nihei) Nihei published a [one-shot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-shot_(comics)) in [Kodansha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kodansha)'s [seinen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seinen_manga) [manga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seinen_manga) magazine [Weekly Young Magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weekly_Young_Magazine) on May 9, 2016[.\[2\]](#page-5-1) In November 2016, it was announced that the manga would be serialized in [Monthly Sh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monthly_Sh%C5%8Dnen_Sirius)ōnen Sirius, being the first time that Nihei would publish a manga in a shō[nen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Dnen_manga) [manga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Dnen_manga) magazine[.\[3\]](#page-5-2) The series ran in the magazine from February 25, 2017[,\[3\]](#page-5-2)[\[4\]](#page-6-0) to August 26, 2021[.\[5\]](#page-6-1) Kodansha collected its chapters in nine [tank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tank%C5%8Dbon)ōbon volumes, released from May 9, 2017[,\[6\]](#page-6-2) to December 9, 2021. [\[7\]](#page-6-3) Kodansha also started publishing a full-color edition of the series on November 8, 2019[,\[8\]](#page-6-4)[\[9\]](#page-6-5) and five volumes have been released as of August 8, 2022[.\[10\]](#page-6-6)

The manga was simultaneously published in English by [Comi Xology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comi Xology) and [Crunchyroll Manga.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crunchyroll_Manga) [\[11\]](#page-6-7)[\[12\]](#page-7-0) In November 2017, [Vertical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertical_(publisher)) announced that they would release the series in print. [\[13\]](#page-7-1) The volumes were released from October 9, 2018[,\[14\]](#page-7-2) to June 28, 2022[.\[15\]](#page-7-3)
