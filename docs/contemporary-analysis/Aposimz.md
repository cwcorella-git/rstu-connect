# *Aposimz*

*Aposimz* (stylized in [all caps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_caps)), known in Japan as *Ningyō no Kuni* (⼈形の国; lit. 'Country of Dolls'), is a Japanese [manga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manga) series written and illustrated by [Tsutomu Nihei.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsutomu_Nihei) It was serialized in [Kodansha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kodansha)'s *[shōnen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Dnen_manga)* [manga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Dnen_manga) magazine *[Monthly Shōnen Sirius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monthly_Sh%C5%8Dnen_Sirius)* from February 2017 to August 2021, with its chapters collected in nine *[tankōbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tank%C5%8Dbon)* volumes.

## **Setting**

The series takes place on an artificial celestial body known as Aposimz (アポシムズ, *Aposhimuzu*), which is 200,000 kilometers in diameter. Most of the planet's volume is occupied by its core, which is covered by a superstructural shell. Five thousand years ago, people who lost a war against the inhabitants of the core were exiled to the surface, a cold and harsh environment. Surface dwellers face both the rapidly spreading Frame Disease (⼈形病, *Ningyō-byō*), which slowly converts normal humans into biomechanical, zombie-like beings, and native biomechanical creatures known as automatons. Certain people have been transformed by special "codes" into Regular Frames (正規⼈形, *Seiki Ningyō*), beings composed of a substance called placenta (胞⾐, *ena*) and fueled by Heigus Particles (ヘイグス粒⼦, *Heigusu Ryūshi*), which renders them biologically immortal and makes them stronger and more resilient than normal humans. Regular Frames are also able to assume an armored form, and most have a unique special ability or weapon. These Regular Frames are highly revered by normal humans and are often leaders and protectors in their communities.

### **Titania** (タイターニア, *Taitānia*)

An intelligent automaton who provides Etherow with the code that transforms him into a Regular Frame as well as seven Anti-Megastructure Bullets (AMBs). She journeys with Etherow as his guide and confidant. Her normal form is that of a small four-legged creature, but she can temporarily assume a humanoid form. #### **Keisha** (ケーシャ, *Kēsha*)

The princess of the underground settlement of Irf Nikk (イルフ‧ニク, *Irufu Niku*) and a Regular Frame who eventually joins Etherow and Titania. Her armored form possesses a staff weapon that can extend to great lengths and is able to channel electricity.

#### **Wasabu** (ワサブ)

A Regular Frame and former member of the Rebedoa Empire's Reincarnated (転⽣者, *Tensei-sha*) who joins Etherow's group. As a Regular Frame, Wasabu is capable of reinforcing himself in armor. He is only able to fly.

## **Publication**

*Aposimz* is written and illustrated by [Tsutomu Nihei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsutomu_Nihei). Nihei published a [one-shot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-shot_(comics)) in [Kodansha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kodansha)'s *[seinen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seinen_manga)* [manga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seinen_manga) magazine *[Weekly Young Magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weekly_Young_Magazine)* on May 9, 2016.[\[2\]](#page-2-1) In November 2016, it was announced that the manga would be serialized in *[Monthly Shōnen Sirius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monthly_Sh%C5%8Dnen_Sirius)*, being the first time that Nihei would publish a manga in a *[shōnen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Dnen_manga)* manga magazine.[\[3\]](#page-2-2) The series ran in the magazine from February 25, 2017,[\[3\]](#page-2-2)[\[4\]](#page-3-0) to August 26, 2021.[\[5\]](#page-3-1) Kodansha collected its 54 chapters in nine *[tankōbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tank%C5%8Dbon)* volumes, released from May 9, 2017,[\[6\]](#page-3-2) to December 9, 2021.[\[7\]](#page-3-3) Kodansha also started publishing a full-color edition of the series on November 8, 2019,[\[8\]](#page-3-4)[\[9\]](#page-3-5) and five volumes have been released as of August 8, 2022.[\[10\]](#page-3-6)

The manga was simultaneously published in English by [Comi Xology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comi Xology) and [Crunchyroll Manga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crunchyroll_Manga). [\[11\]](#page-3-7)[\[12\]](#page-3-8) In November 2017, [Vertical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertical_(publisher)) announced that they would release the series in print.[\[13\]](#page-3-9) The volumes were released from October 9, 2018,[\[14\]](#page-3-10) to June 28, 2022.[\[15\]](#page-4-0)
