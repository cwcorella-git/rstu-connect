# DEVELOPMENT AND DEMONSTRATION TEST FOR FLOATING TYPE OCEAN CURRENT TURBINE SYSTEM CONDUCTED IN KUROSHIO CURRENT

Masayuki Shimizu\* Shigeki Nagaya\* Tomohiro Ueno\* Hiroyuki Saito\* Show Murata\* Akio Ito\* \* IHI corporation, Kanagawa 235-8501, Japan

Kuroshio current flowing near coastal areas of Japan is one of the strongest ocean currents, and IHI is developing the floating type ocean current turbine system. In this study, we have carried out technical development of the floating type ocean current turbine system, engineering, fabrication, integration, and commissioning of 100kW turbine demonstration machine. After that, we implemented demonstration test on August-2017. As a result, the power generation from Kuroshio current was achieved, and we collected data for future practical applications, such as performance validation of the autonomous control system that stabilizes its underwater position

Keywords: marine renewable energy, offshore structure, power generation

### INTRODUCTION

Japan's Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) extending outside Japanese territorial waters is the world's sixth largest EEZ. Proceeding development of utilizing ocean renewable energy in the EEZ actively is required in terms of the reduction in greenhouse gas emissions and energy security. Ocean current known as Kuroshio Current flows stably almost all year in the vicinity of Japanese coastal area. Accordingly, by using this Japanese own natural enormous energy, it is strongly expected to establish new clean and stable source of electric power. In order to realize power generation utilizing the Kuroshio Current, we are developing the floating type ocean current turbine system.

### METHOD

## Technical Concept

Our ocean current turbine shown in Fig. 1 has two major features.

- ・Mooring In this system, the floating turbines are moored to the seabed so that they remain suspended in the ocean. They can even be installed in deep water, which means that a wide ocean area is available for power generation using this system. Therefore, it allows us to construct a large-scale energy farm consisting of a number of power generators.
- ・Counter-rotating Twin Turbines Rotary torque generated by turbine rotation can be canceled by coupling two counter-rotating underwater turbines. This will allow the turbines to maintain a stable position under the sea to achieve efficient power generation.

In addition to these two features, ocean current turbines will be used in the middle layer of the sea, which makes influence on the motion caused by waves much smaller. Also, due to the downwind type, this system can be applied in any current direction without active control.

#### B. Components

- Buoyancy adjustment device; ballast water management controls the weight of KAIRYU.
- Attitude adjustment device; ballast shifting among three pods changes the position of center of gravity to prevent trim and heal from inclining.

- Generator; permanent magnet synchronous generators can make variable torque, which controls the turbine rotation speed at any current velocity.
- Blade pitch control device; pitch angles of 4 turbine blades can be controlled independently.
- Mooring line; HMPE rope has high strength, low elongation and lightness.
- Power cable; Electricity is transmitted through riser cable and subsea cable. They also include optical fiber cable, therefore monitoring of signals from each instrument and actuator is possible at the shore side.

### C. Control

#### 1) Depth

It is required to control depth in various operating phases such as submerging from the sea surface, maintaining depth in the operational range, and surfacing for maintenance.

The depth of KAIRYU is determined by the balance of vertical forces, which consist of the gravity, buoyancy, lift force and mooring tension. To control its depth, KAIRYU adds or decreases its gravity by means of the buoyancy adjustment device. Additionally, the mooring tension is affected by the turbine thrust force, so adding or decreasing thrust force by turbine pitch angle is another method of controlling depth.

## 2) Attitude (Heal/Trim)

It is necessary to maintain heal and trim angle almost even. Attitude is controlled by attitude adjustment device. By shifting ballast among three pods, heal and trim angle of KAIRYU can be controlled at the same time.

As for yawing, KAIRYU follows ocean current direction without any control, which is given by weather vane function of single point mooring.

### 3) Power generation

In order to prevent the generators from getting overload, the generated power has to be kept below the rated value at high velocity. The generated power can be controlled with the turbine blade pitch angle. When the generated power exceeds the threshold, blade pitch angle will be changed and power generation efficiency will become lower. As a result, the power generation can be decreased.

These controls are conducted automatically.

#### RESULT

### Power Generation

Power generation performance at various velocity ranges was obtained in the towing test. The relation between power generation and current velocity in the towing test is shown in Fig. 14. The obtained result is well coincided with the designed performance.

Also, the KAIRYU generated approximately 30kW output in electric power at velocity of approximately 1.0m/s from the natural ocean current in mooring test.

### Floating stability in the Kuroshio Current

In the demonstration test, we confirmed the stability of depth and attitude under the Kuroshio current.

Result of time history about depth and attitude, when the operation phase changed from waiting mode at sea surface to power generating mode in the mooring test, are shown in . According the result, we can confirm that KAIRYU has the high following capability of depth to command value, and that maximum angle of attitude incline was kept within approximately 5 degree, which means high stability.

Throughout the mooring test, KAIRYU could control its depth among the target range and attitude within ±10 degree. This high stability comes from the positional relation between the center of gravity and the center of buoyancy. Thus, active control by the attitude adjustment device was not used continuously but only when needed.

#### Deployment

For the mooring test, KAIRYU was carried to near Kuchinoshima and installed with gravity base anchor, mooring line and power cable. The process of installation including laying down the subsea cable was completed in 3 days. This quick installation was achieved by the simple mooring configuration of this floating type power generation system.
