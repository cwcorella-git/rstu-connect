The **Galactic Republic**, also known as the **Grand Republic** or simply as the **Republic**, was a [democratic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Democracy) [federal union](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Politics#Republic) spread across [light-years](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Light-year) of [space.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Realspace) **Old Republic** and **first Galactic Republic** were terms affixed to the pre-modern Republic that existed until [1032 BBY,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/1032_BBY) with the Old Republic also referring to the modern Republic that was supplanted by the [Galactic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Empire) [Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Empire) in [19 BBY.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/19_BBY) In addition, the term **High Republic** denoted the [era](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/High_Republic_Era) in which the Republic reached the zenith of its power, lasting from around [500 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/500_BBY) to around [100 BBY.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/100_BBY) The Republic was founded many [millennia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Millennium) before the [Battle of Yavin,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Yavin) approximately [25,000 BBY,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/25,000_BBY) around the same [time](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Time) as the [Jedi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order)

[Order.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order) The [Galactic Senate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Senate) governed the Republic under the [Galactic Constitution,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Constitution) and comprised thousands of [senators](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senator) representing the Republic's member worlds. The [Supreme](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Chancellor) [Chancellor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Chancellor) was the title of the head of the Republic, elected from ranks of the Senate to lead the government on [Coruscant.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant) An [ecumenopolis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ecumenopolis) or [city-](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/City)covered [planet,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Planet) referred to as [Galactic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_City) [City,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_City) Coruscant was the [capital](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Capital) of the Republic and therefore the [seat of the Senate.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Senate_Building)

The Galactic Republic and it [star systems](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_system) shared sovereignty, and spanned a multiplicity of worlds from the [Core Worlds](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Core_Worlds) to the [Outer Rim Territories,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Rim_Territories) uniting a variety of [sentient](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sentience) [species,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Species) [human](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human) and [alien](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alien) alike, under [one banner.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_crest) As its power grew, the Republic fought many wars against various opponents such as [Mandalorians,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian) the [Zygerrian Slave Empire,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zygerrian_Slave_Empire) the [Nihil,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nihil) and several [Sith Empires.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire) The [Jedi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi) Order, a [religious](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Religion) group of [Force](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force-sensitive)[sensitives](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force-sensitive) dedicated to the [light side of the Force,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Light_side_of_the_Force) was sworn to the service of the Senate, and as such the [Jedi Knights](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Knight) were hailed as the guardians of peace and justice within the Republic. [Centuries](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Standard_century) of conflict culminated in the [fall of the Old Republic,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fall_of_the_Old_Republic) and the [last war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi-Sith_War) between the ancient Jedi and [Sith](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith) concluded with the restoration and rise of the modern Galactic Republic, ushering in the [Republic Era](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Era) which was an age of peace that lasted for a thousand [years.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Standard_year) The Republic's [history](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline_of_galactic_history) was officially reset, and the ages before this event were merged into a collective whole known as the [Old Republic-era.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Old_Republic-era)

The Republic entered a period of decline after centuries of expansion and prosperity. Aided by the [resurgent](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rule_of_Two) [Sith Lords,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Lord) the [Separatist Crisis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Separatist_Crisis) divided the galaxy between the Republic [loyalists](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Loyalist) and the [Confederacy of Independent Systems.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Confederacy_of_Independent_Systems) The [Grand Army of the Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Army_of_the_Republic) was organized under the leadership of [Jedi Generals](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_General) who led the [Republic Military](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Military) against the

[Separatist Droid Army](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Separatist_Droid_Army) during the [Clone Wars.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_Wars) However, the conflict had been [orchestrated](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Plan) to allow Supreme Chancellor [Sheev Palpatine—](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Sidious)the alter ego of [Darth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth) Sidious, the [Dark Lord](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Lord_of_the_Sith) [of the Sith—](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Lord_of_the_Sith)to control and deceive the Republic in a [dictatorial](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dictatorship) manner. With the [end](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Utapau) of the war, Sidious initiated the [Great Jedi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Jedi_Purge) [Purge](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Jedi_Purge) through [Order 66](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Order_66) and transformed the Republic into the Galactic Empire by [declaring](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Proclamation_of_the_New_Order) himself [Galactic Emperor.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Emperor) The [Imperial Senate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Senate) stood as the last remnant of the Old Republic until it was [disbanded](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dissolution_of_the_Imperial_Senate) by the [Emperor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emperor) at the height of the [Imperial Era.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Era) In the aftermath of the [Galactic Civil War,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Civil_War) the [Alliance to Restore the Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alliance_to_Restore_the_Republic) was fully [reorganized](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Declaration_of_a_New_Republic) into the [New Republic,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Republic) restoring the Galactic Republic after a generation of [Imperial rule.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fascism)

The rogue faction was ultimately [defeated,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_war_(Old_Republic)) and they were [exiled](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Coruscant_(Sith_war)) from the Old Republic into uncharted [space.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Realspace) [\[5\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Tarkin-5)

At some point, the [Jedi Temple](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Palace) was constructed over the Sith shrine in order to contain its dark energy[.\[5\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Tarkin-5) Once it had established itself on Coruscant, the Jedi Order closely associated itself with the Old Republic, serving as guardians of the peace and justice for over a thousand generations.[\[18\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_IV-18) Little did the Jedi know, the Sith had regrouped on the planet [Moraband.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moraband) [\[26\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Sith-26)

As the Old Republic spread its influence across the [stars,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star) it came into contact with various other powers, one of them being the [Zygerrian Slave Empire.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zygerrian_Slave_Empire) Due to the fact that, at some point in its past, the Old Republic declared [slavery](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Slavery) illegal and expected other civilizations of the galaxy to comply, it took an aggressive stance against the [Zygerrians.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Zygerrian) The Zygerrian Empire, which was built upon slavery, refused to comply, forcing both the Old Republic and the Jedi to intervene. ## ![](_page_6_Picture_2.jpeg)

Destructive wars were fought between the Sith and the Jedi in the age of the Old Republic.

Eventually, the Sith would return in full strength, invading the Old Republic. From there, the dark Order would do battle with the Jedi and the Old Republic. This would lead to a series of conflicts between both groups that would go on for thousands of years until the Old Republic's ultimate collapse.[\[26\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Sith-26) During one of the wars, the Jedi [fought](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Scourge_of_Malachor) the [Sith Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Empire) on [Malachor,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Malachor) which proved to be one of the most devastating battles in the history of the Order[.\[30\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Twilight_of_the_Apprentice-30)

At some point, the Old Republic came into contact with the [Mandalorian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian) people. The warrior civilization began a series of unprovoked military [campaigns](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian_crusades) against the Old Republic. Later on, after all-out [war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalorian-Jedi_War) erupted, the Jedi stepped in to defend the Republic[.\[31\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-The_Mandalore_Plot-31) In the meantime, Republic and Jedi forces continued to wage war against the expanding Sith Empire. During these wars, both sides would construct massive [superweapons](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Superweapon) powered by large [kyber crystals](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kyber_crystal) capable of mass destruction[.\[32\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-The_Big_Bang-32)[\[33\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Death_Star_Superlaser-33)

The [Middle Era of the Old Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Middle_Era_of_the_Old_Republic) was one period of galactic history[.\[34\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Guardians_of_the_Whills-34) The Galactic Republic also participated in the [Sith](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Wars) [Wars,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Wars) a series of conflicts against the Sith Empire and involving the [Chiss Ascendancy.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chiss_Ascendancy) The Ascendancy encountered a number of [aliens](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alien) before retreating to their borders[.\[22\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Chaos_Rising-22)

The Mandalorian Wars brought on the beginning of the Old Republic's downfall.

With the civilization of the Sith and the warrior culture of [Mandalore,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mandalore) and with their beliefs of continual warfare without regard to the Jedi mandate of protecting the weak, the Jedi Order continued to mobilize its forces in an effort to stop enemy incursions into Old Republic space[.\[31\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-The_Mandalore_Plot-31)[\[26\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Sith-26)

The power of the Sith Empire increased during the [fall of the Old](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fall_of_the_Old_Republic) [Republic.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fall_of_the_Old_Republic) The Sith soon achieved near-dominance across the galaxy[.\[2\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_III-2) It was also during this time that an ancestor of [Pre](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pre_Vizsla) [Vizsla,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pre_Vizsla) [\[35\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Rebels_Recon_#3.14-35) leader of the Mandalorian group [Death Watch](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Watch) during the [Clone Wars,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_Wars) managed to steal the [Darksaber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darksaber) from the Jedi [Temple.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_temple) [\[31\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-The_Mandalore_Plot-31)

At some point the galaxy entered a state known as the Dark Age. During this time, the [Jedi-Sith War](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi-Sith_War) broke out. Early on in this conflict, the Old Republic capital world of Coruscant was taken over by the Sith during the [Battle of Coruscant.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Coruscant_(Jedi-Sith_War)) Later on in the war, the Sith were driven off the planet by the Jedi during the [Liberation of Coruscant.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Liberation_of_Coruscant) [\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)

The modern Republic was a [democratic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Democracy) union that consisted of multiple [star systems](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_system) spread across the galaxy[.\[1\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-RepublicDB-1) With the exception of small-scale conflicts that the Jedi dealt with, the Republic Era was an age of peace and stability that lasted for over a [millennium.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Millennium) [\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15) This version of the Republic existed in a demilitarized state for most of its history[;\[9\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_II-9) as such, the government relied on the [Jedi Knights,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Knight) who upheld their mandate as the guardians of peace and justice.[\[47\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-JediDB-47)

The [Four Sages of Dwartii](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Four_Sages_of_Dwartii) were notable in influencing some of its earliest laws[.\[48\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-VE-48) As its governing body, the newly reorganized government established the [Galactic Senate,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Senate) the members of which were elected to represent their systems. Coruscant was the galactic seat of power under the Republic,[\[1\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-RepublicDB-1) as it had been for thousands of years before the Republic's rebirth[.\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)

The Republic's head of state, the [Supreme Chancellor,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Chancellor) was elected by and from the senators, in which the first in a long line of Chancellor[s\[5\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Tarkin-5) was [Tarsus Valorum,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tarsus_Valorum) [\[8\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Rise_of_the_Separatists-8) a scion of the [House](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/House_Valorum) [Valorum.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/House_Valorum) [\[5\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Tarkin-5)[\[10\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_I-10) Protecting the new democracy was the elite [Senate Guard,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senate_Guard) who could be seen patrolling the [Senate District](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Federal_District) and came to be seen as symbols of strength and unity, especially considering the fact that the military had been disbanded[.\[5\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Tarkin-5)[\[49\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWEsenateguard-49) In time, the Jedi Order, a noble order of protectors who could tap into the power of the Force, came to serve the Republic as guardians of peace and justice.[\[47\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-JediDB-47) With the lack of a standing army, the Republic came to rely on its peacekeeping [Judicial Forces,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Judicial_Forces) led by the Judicial Department as the de facto [law enforcement](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Law_enforcement_agency) branch of the government. The Judicial Forces, members of which were simply known as [Judicials,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Judicial) trained at the [Judicial Academy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Judicial_Academy) and came to serve in both ground and space forces, often led by Jedi [Commanders](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Commander) to maintain peace throughout the galaxy[.\[5\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Tarkin-5)

## ![](_page_23_Picture_2.jpeg)

Supreme Chancellor Lina Soh endeavored to unite the various peoples of the Galactic Republic.

In [232 BBY](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/232_BBY)[\[70\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-HR_Announcement_date-70) the Galactic Republic was under the leadership of Supreme Chancellor [Lina Soh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lina_Soh)[\[6\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Light_of_the_Jedi-6) and Vice Chancellor [Larep](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Larep_Reza) [Reza,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Larep_Reza) who was a rising star in the Senate and thought to be Soh's apparent successor whenever her term came to its end. [\[63\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Rising_Storm-63) To showcase support for all species in its borders, the governmental motto became "We are all the Republic." Under Soh[,\[6\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Light_of_the_Jedi-6) the Republic even began to take steps to curb its habit of indulging in bureaucracy when it needed to be instead taking action; even though the issue remained in a lesser form, the Republic took great steps in advancing beyond it.[\[71\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Midnight_Horizon-71) At some point the Republic intervened in the [Soikan civil war](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Soikan_conflict) to finally put a stop to the seemingly-endless conflict[.\[68\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Mission_to_Disaster-68)

Concurrent to its [outreach program,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outreach_program) the Republic was in the midst of an era where all voices were listened to, creating governance from consensus and a society of culture, inclusion, ambition, and Soh's many [Great Works.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Works) Among the earliest of these works—which encompassed everything from infrastructure projects to cultural affairs— was the [Panacea](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Panacea), a [Salvation](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Salvation-class_Republic_medical_frigate)[-class Republic medical frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Salvation-class_Republic_medical_frigate) that was deployed to help citizens after various crises; therefore, the [frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Frigate) became a symbol of Republic's dedication to help its citizens. Other planned Great Works included a new treaty between the [Mon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mon_Calamari) [Calamari](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mon_Calamari) and [Quarren,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quarren) the [Republic Fair,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Fair) various communication relays, cementing the understanding of how to cultivate [Bacta,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bacta) and various innovations in fields like technology. The greatest of the works was nonetheless [Starlight](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Starlight_Beacon) [Beacon,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Starlight_Beacon) which was planned to only be the first in a Beacon network that could truly bring the Republic out of the Core.[\[6\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Light_of_the_Jedi-6)

## ![](_page_24_Picture_3.jpeg)

The Republic's "Great Works" initiative saw the construction of distant outposts like Starlight Beacon.

Constructed along with other way stations at considerable expense and effor[t\[69\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SW_2-69) through many challenges over a long period of time[,\[6\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Light_of_the_Jedi-6) Starlight Beacon was designed to expand the Republic's domain to the unexplored "dark zones" of the [Outer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Rim_Territories) [Rim Territories](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Rim_Territories)[\[69\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SW_2-69) because Soh pledged to bring that region into the Republic's embrace, hoping the government could actually help the Outer Rim instead of simply flying through it or using it as another place to collect taxes. Beyond using its beacon to guide travelers and its relay to boost local transmissions by a factor of ten, the Beacon was a Republic embassy[\[6\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Light_of_the_Jedi-6) within the Galactic Frontier[,\[72\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Great_Jedi_Rescue-72) that could serve many other purposes, being a fortress to discourage piracy, a place for cultural showcases, a state-of-the-art medical center, and, in part, a massive [Jedi temple.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Starlight_Beacon%27s_Jedi_temple) The Jedi provided support to the Republic and its projects. The Jedi established outposts on many Outer Rim worlds, ensuring they could explore and protect those who lived without much of a Republic presence[.\[6\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Light_of_the_Jedi-6)

Although the Outer Rim was hard to navigate and dangerous to live in[,\[69\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SW_2-69) many poor settlers traveled to the region, leaving the overpopulated Core Worlds and Colonies in pursuit of new lives. These dreams for better lives were supported by the Republic, which—although imperfect like all governments—encouraged the hopes of its citizens, whether those hopes be large or small. Additionally, Soh wanted the people of the Republic to know that they all were part of the government, and the Republic began construction of Starlight Beacon to help with its expansion into the Outer Rim. The beacon was the symbol of everything Soh wanted for the Republic, being able to ensure it could truly expand from the Core to be a galactic government. Others in the government preferred the status quo, wanting to save credits in case of future issues, but Soh, having proved to her people the positive effect of coming together, pushed on with her works to bring the Republic into the Rim. Every Senator knew the success of Starlight would bring forth Soh's hopes that future generations could live in prosperity, while its failure would be massive[.\[6\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Light_of_the_Jedi-6)

#### **Expansion and stagnation[**

**[\]](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Republic?veaction=edit§ion=12)**

"With eyes toward expansion into the uncharted reaches of the Outer Rim, the traditions of the Core became passé. Opportunity beckoned from beyond the borders of the Mid Rim worlds."

―Janyo[r\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)

## ![](_page_33_Picture_7.jpeg)

## ![](_page_34_Picture_2.jpeg)

Commerce guilds generated massive profits by investing in the Republic's expansionist policy.

For centuries, the Galactic Republic expanded not through force but by quietly exerting a strong magnetic pull towards neighboring systems. The promise of trade with [Core World](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Core_Worlds) markets held many systems in sway, inexorably luring nonmember worlds into tighter cooperation with the state body. Despite this, the Republic remained slow to invite new systems into its fold, as the addition of new territories diminished the [political](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Politics) power of existing senators. New member states invariably aligned themselves with local galactic power blocs, while most senators who gave the invitations represented systems in the Core. As a result, the Galactic Center represented the beating heart of the Republic that habitually drew wealth and power from the hinterlands, with most senators dismaying at the extension of services and protection to outlying worlds. Consequently, many useful star systems were left waiting—some for centuries—for the body politic to admit their world as a member of the Congress, even though it came at the cost of the body's overall power[.\[79\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-A_New_Dawn-79)

As time went by the Republic stood firm, becoming increasingly powerful. As a result, the galaxy remained free of any full-scale war for centuries. However, many of the [bureaucrats](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bureaucrat) and [senators](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senator) that ran the government continued to work for their own profit rather than the common good. Greed, corruption and internal strife slowly began destroying the government from within[.\[1\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-RepublicDB-1) The Core, revitalized from the destructive conflicts of the past and ravenous for new resources to exploit, embarked on an aggressive colonization policy, exploring, mapping, and settling untouched planets within the Outer Systems, striking deals with or simply overrunning the indigenous populations that stood in their path. Adventurous pioneers found themselves granted permission by Coruscant to settle new territories and establish trading colonies to feed the Core with the goods and raw materials for its continual self-enrichment. Many new systems, finding themselves lacking the funds to mine, process, and ship their products to market, were forced to secure high-interest loans from the [Inter Galactic Banking Clan,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Inter Galactic_Banking_Clan) whereupon many, unable to repay their debts, found themselves as mere client worlds to the [Muun](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Muun) bankers. As time progressed, the laborious task of astrogating by hyperwave beacons—which required numerous reversions to [realspace](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Realspace) became simplified with the founding of new [hyperlanes,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyperspace_route) such as the [Hydian Way.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hydian_Way) Many systems, eager to benefit from the opportunities offered by charting hyperlanes through their system, sought to influence the Republic Senate to put themselves on the galactic map[.\[5\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Tarkin-5)

## ![](_page_35_Picture_3.jpeg)

The peoples of various worlds were influenced by Coruscanti culture and Republic architecture during the reign of the Galactic Republic.

Culturally, [Coruscanti](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscanti) fashion, art, drama and literature saw receptive audiences across the galaxy. Seeking to impress visitors from the Core, many worlds sought to build lavish mansions that imitated the [architectural style](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_architecture) of the Inner Core. Despite this, many Coruscanti looked with disdain on what was in reality nothing more than a gaudy impersonation. To them, these lifeforms on the galaxy's fringes appeared barbaric, with most worlds still lacking weather control or struggling with geological disturbances and food shortages. Additionally, many outlying worlds, lacking the protection of a federal military found themselves continually harassed by numerous [pirates](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pirate) and criminal organizations, further ingraining the long-held view that those from the galaxy's outlying settlements lacked cultural refinement and basic civility[.\[5\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Tarkin-5)

A deep-rooted hatred and distrust grew over the centuries between the galaxy's outer fringes and the cosmopolitan Core, with those in the hinterlands gradually coming to believe themselves the victims of social and economic injustices. As a result of the perceived inability of the Judicial Forces in protecting outlying territories, who were often withheld in intervening after many far-flung worlds refused to provide the Core Worlds with profitable deals, the Outer Rim world of [Eriadu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eriadu)

—one of the most politically, culturally and economically developed worlds—formed the [Outland Regions Security Force](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outland_Regions_Security_Force) for the protection of the [Seswenna sector.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Seswenna_sector) Comprising an amalgam of ships, it was funded primarily by off-world loans and supplied with [laser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Laser) and [ion cannons](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ion_cannon) acquired from arms merchants who had for centuries been ignoring a Republic ban on the sale of weaponry to member worlds. While the Outland Regions Security Force at first lacked success, it soon came to be renowned for its efficiency at dealing with raiders, especially after [Wilhuff Tarkin](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Wilhuff_Tarkin) was accepted into Outland's anti-piracy task force, in which he was known for outsmarting and outmaneuvering his opponents[.\[5\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Tarkin-5)

The Galactic Senate was mired in political corruption during the Republic's final years.

The Separatist Crisis reflected widespread disillusionment with the Republic.

In his wake, Palpatine was appointed to the position of Supreme Chancellor, eventually creating an image of himself as a mild-mannered servant of the common good, despite his true intentions. The Battle of Geonosis was the first battle in the Clone Wars.

The Clone Wars began with the [First Battle of Geonosis.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_Battle_of_Geonosis)

Supported by the [Republic Navy,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Navy) the Grand Army of the Republic engaged the [Separatist Droid Army](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Separatist_Droid_Army) in the open field. The Jedi joined the battle as well[,\[9\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_II-9) acting as de facto generals for the clone troopers[.\[82\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SW:CL-82) Meanwhile, [Poggle the Lesser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Poggle_the_Lesser)'s schematics for the [Ultimate Weapon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DS-1_Orbital_Battle_Station) were transferred to Dooku who escaped the battle after [dueling](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_duel) Skywalker, Kenobi, and [Grand Master](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Master) [Yoda.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yoda) Simultaneously, the various members of the newly-founded [Confederacy of Independent Systems](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Confederacy_of_Independent_Systems) evacuated the planet as Republic clone [troopers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trooper) gradually forced the droid army into full retreat. The battle was hailed as a Republic victory, although Yoda took the opposite position as he knew that war would spread across the galaxy. Count Dooku traveled to an [industrial sector](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Works) on Coruscant to inform his master that the war had begun as planned, all while the Jedi High Council pondered Dooku's warning that the Dark Lord of the Sith controlled the Senate[.\[9\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_II-9)

In the aftermath of the Battle of [Geonosis,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Geonosis) the deadly three-year conflict that engulfed the galaxy in chaos officially began.[\[25\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-TSWB-25) Though suspicious of Dooku's claim that the Senate was under the Sith's influence[,\[9\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_II-9) the Jedi continued to serve the Republic. The [military rank](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Military_rank) of [Jedi General](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_General) was created[,\[83\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Mace_Windu_1-83) allowing the Jedi to lead the Grand Army of the Republic in an official capacity[.\[25\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-TSWB-25) Early into the war, Sidious learned of a splinter faction in the Trade Federation under the Neimoidian [Oje](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oje_N%27deeb) [N'deeb,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Oje_N%27deeb) who wanted to usurp Gunray and give the Outer Rim a chance for a new future. While initially outraged and considering crushing the rebellion outright to ensure the Federation and the legacy it would leave behind stayed true to his plans, Sidious quickly realized he could use the upstart to his advantage; if N'deeb's operation was turned to his own ends, the Outer Rim would be strengthened by N'deeb's faction, but that work would only serve to prolong the war. Once the "chance" N'deeb offered the Rim was crushed, furthermore, Sidious understood that its people who feel even more bitter towards the Republic[.\[84\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-QH-84)

Chancellor Palpatine's wartime powers made him a dictator in all but name.

The war also served as a means for Palpatine to amass more emergency powers, cementing his position as Supreme Chancellor far longer than it technically allowed, all while continuing to centralize political and military authority within the [Office of the Chancellor,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Office_of_the_Chancellor) which included putting the Inter Galactic Banking Clan under the Chancellor's oversight.[\[4\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Crisis_at_the_Heart-4) By gaining wartime powers, Chancellor Palpatine was effectively a [dictator.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dictatorship) [\[101\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Darth_Sidious_DB-101)

Palpatine used the war as his reasoning for amending the Galactic Constitution multiple times, enshrining greater power in his office through his changes[.\[102\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Rebel_Files-102) He became the most powerful chancellor since before the [Ruusan Reformations.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ruusan_Reformations) Under the [Reflex Amendment,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Reflex_Amendment) he secured near-total power over the military[.\[103\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-CotR-103) The [Sector Governance Decree,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sector_Governance_Decree) passed towards the end of the war, gave [governors](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Governor) sent out by Palpatine authority over neighboring star systems, all in the name of the war effort[.\[104\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-OF84-104)

## ![](_page_56_Picture_2.jpeg)

The Republic invaded [Utapau](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Utapau) with the goal of bringing a decisive end to the Clone Wars.

After nearly three years of constant warfare, the war reached its climax during the [Battle of Coruscant,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Coruscant) where both factions engaged in a massive naval and ground battle over the Republic's capital world. The confrontation ultimately saw the Republic dealing a severe blow to the Confederacy with the loss of a portion of its fleet as well as its head of state, Count Dooku, at the hands of Anakin Skywalker. Shortly afterwards, [General](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/General) [Grievous](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grievous) met his end at the hands of Obi-Wan Kenobi during the [Battle of Utapau.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Utapau) Yet even as the Republic pressed on with the Outer Rim Sieges, determined to bring an end to the civil war, the Jedi High Council had grown increasingly wary of Palpatine. [\[2\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_III-2)

Over the course of the Clone Wars, the Chancellor's wartime authority reached unprecedented levels at the expense of the Senate, some of whom also grew concerned for the future of the Republic. As such, members of the Council and the Senate began to consider their options for the preservation of democracy[.\[2\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_III-2) The [Delegation of 2,000](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Delegation_of_2,000) emerged and sought to [petition](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Petition_of_2,000) the Chancellor to relinquish his emergency powers,[\[105\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Meena_Tills-105) whereas the Council contemplated the possibility of removing him by force and taking over the Senate in order to secure a peaceful transition of power[.\[2\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_III-2)

## ![](_page_57_Picture_3.jpeg)

Palpatine was the Sith Lord Darth Sidious, whose machinations brought about the fall of the Jedi and the Republic.

Ultimately, their suspicion of the Chancellor was vindicated when Skywalker uncovered the truth: Sheev Palpatine and Darth Sidious, the Sith mastermind behind the Clone Wars, were one and the same. Realizing they had been deceived by the Chancellor, the Council [attempted to arrest](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_in_Palpatine%27s_office) the Sith Lord before his plan could come to fruition, consequently leading to a [lightsaber duel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber_duel) in which Sidious struck down [Agen Kolar,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Agen_Kolar) [Saesee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saesee_Tiin) [Tiin,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Saesee_Tiin) and [Kit Fisto](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kit_Fisto) before [Mace Windu](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mace_Windu) disarmed him. Given the Chancellor's control over the Senate and the courts, Windu deemed Sidious too dangerous to live. However, Skywalker, despite having performed an execution himself, argued that execution was contrary to the Jedi way, though his personal attachment to Amidala also influenced him at this critical point as he believed that Sidious could save her life.[\[2\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_III-2)

In a moment of desperation, Skywalker disarmed Windu, which allowed Sidious to kill the Jedi Order's champion with a torrent of [Force lightning.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_lightning) Having chosen the path of the dark side, the fallen Jedi Knight became Darth Vader and subsequently led the [attack on the Jedi Temple](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Operation:_Knightfall) while Sidious issued [Order 66](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Order_66) to the Grand Army, causing the clone [troopers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trooper) being [brainwashed](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Behavioral_modification_biochip) into betraying and executing their Jedi leaders across the galaxy.[\[2\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_III-2) The execution of Order 66 marked the moment where the destruction of the Republic was inevitable[,\[106\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-BB_S2-106) although Maul maintained that the Republic had already fallen and the Jedi had simply failed to realize as such[.\[107\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-TPA-107)

## ![](_page_58_Picture_4.jpeg)

With the Jedi eliminated, Sidious declared himself Emperor of the Galactic Empire.

The Senate held an emergency session at the Chancellor's request. Sidious publicly denounced the Jedi Order; he accused them of plotting to assassinate him in order to take control of the Senate, and by extension, the Republic. Meanwhile, Vader undertook a [mission to Mustafar](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mission_to_Mustafar) where he personally executed the [Separatist Council,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Executive_Separatist_Council) at the behest of Sidious who betrayed and no longer needed them. Historians rebuked the generation that witnessed the Republic's downfall, holding the people of this last era of peace responsible for the death of liberty.

Hailed as one of the greatest political achievements in the galaxy's history[,\[25\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-TSWB-25) the Galactic Republic was described by the historian [Janyor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Janyor) as "the Republic of legend, greater than distance or time." Although other versions existed in the ancient past, collectively known as the "Old Republic," posterity credited the modern Republic for delivering the galaxy from an age of darkness and, with the help of the Jedi Order, forging an era of peace that lasted for a thousand years until the advent of the Clone Wars. Historians romanticized the Age of the Republic, calling it the "last era of peace"—notwithstanding isolated conflicts, which were notably few and swiftly resolved by Jedi mediators—before the rise of vast warships and planetkilling superweapons[.\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)

The Republic in its final years, however, was remembered with less fondness. Historians regarded the people of that time as a generation of apathy and indolence, in which galactic senators cared more about power and luxury than representing their constituents or addressing the perceived inefficiencies of the government. The civilian population was also seen as a contributing factor to the Republic's demise, with Core Worlders being more invested in podracing events in the distant Outer Rim Territories than the politics that caused dissatisfaction to fester and grow into the Separatist Crisis. Ultimately, historians rebuked this generation and held it responsible for the collapse of the "comfortable civilization" that defined the modern Galactic Republic[.\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)

## ![](_page_66_Picture_5.jpeg)

The Supreme Chancellor was the elected leader of the Galactic Senate.

The institutions of the Galactic Republic consisted of the Office of the Chancellor, the Galactic Senate, and the Judicial Department. Executive power was vested in the Supreme Chancellor, although this office was largely powerless and hampered by bureaucratic procedure, particularly during the chancellery of Finis Valorum. Nevertheless, the Chancellor had the authority to issue executive orders summoning the Senate to an emergency sessio[n\[10\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_I-10) or sending senators away from Coruscant for security reasons[.\[9\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_II-9)

The chancellery of Sheev Palpatine shifted the balance of power in favor of the Chancellor's office, a consequence of the wartime powers that the Senate gave its leader on the eve of the Clone Wars[.\[9\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_II-9) As the conflict spread across the galaxy, Palpatine's chancellery gradually morphed into a dictatorship[\[101\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Darth_Sidious_DB-101) with control over both the Senate and the [Republic courts.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_courts) Near the war's end, Palpatine appointed Anakin Skywalker as his own personal representative on the Jedi High Council, which the Jedi considered an overreaching exercise of the Chancellor's authority. By then, Palpatine had managed to remain in power by exceeding the term limits of his office, an action that the Senate supported[.\[2\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_III-2)

## ![](_page_67_Picture_4.jpeg)

The Galactic Senate was the governing body of the Republic.

Prior to Palpatine's rise to power, the Galactic Senate was the legislative body of the Republic. Many member worlds across the galaxy were represented in the Senate which was headquartered on Coruscant, capital of the Republic.[\[119\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Senate_DB-119) At it height, the Senate's power eclipsed that of the Supreme Chancello[r\[10\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_I-10) but it had also become stagnant under unbridled corruption and layers of bureaucracy[.\[119\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Senate_DB-119) As more politicians grew enamored with the comforts of their office, prioritizing power and wealth over their constituents' needs, average citizens questioned if the Republic and its high taxes were worth the membership[.\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15) The Jedi had also grown distrustful towards senators and regarded their motives with skepticism,[\[9\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_II-9) notwithstanding the Order's allegiance to the Senate as a whole[.\[2\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_III-2)

## ![](_page_68_Picture_3.jpeg)

Conflicting ideologies led to factionalism, intrigue, and murder in the Senate.

With the Senate hamstrung by political infighting and a strict adherence to protocol, it failed to bring swift resolutions to either the Invasion of Nabo[o\[10\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_I-10) or the Separatist Crisis[.\[9\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_II-9) In lieu of a diplomatic solution, the Senate rallied under Chancellor Palpatine's leadership. However, the decision to grant him emergency powers eroded their authority, allowing Palpatine to amass even more power at the expense of the Senate. At the time of the Clone Wars, the Senate was divided between supporters of the war effort and pacifists who sought a peaceful end to the fighting[.\[119\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Senate_DB-119)

The Judicial Department was an umbrella department over the Republic courts, including the [Supreme Court,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Court) the highest court of law in the Republic[.\[8\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Rise_of_the_Separatists-8) Its authority also extended over the Judicial Force[s\[5\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Tarkin-5) and the [Republic Office of Criminal](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Office_of_Criminal_Investigations) [Investigations.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Office_of_Criminal_Investigations) [\[8\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Rise_of_the_Separatists-8) Like its legislative counterpart, however, the courts were mired by a stagnant bureaucracy and was known to be even more indecisive than the Senate[.\[10\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_I-10) Despite the arrest of Nute Gunray, multiple trials in the Supreme Court failed to remove him as leader of the Trade Federation to the dismay of the Naboo government[.\[9\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_II-9)

The Republic Defense Coalition was an interplanetary alliance between the Republic's member worlds.

The Republic Defense Coalition, also known as the RDC, was organized as a small peacekeeping fleet that defended the Galactic Republic's interests during the High Republic Era. The RDC was bound together as an organization through defense treaties signed by the Republic's member worlds, including Alderaan and Chandrila. The treaties that created the RDC gave it the ability to allocate starships and personnel from the signatory worlds in times of emergency. These resources would remain under the RDC's control until the threat was neutralized, after which they would be returned to their homeworlds.[\[6\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Light_of_the_Jedi-6) The Republic also maintained formations of Republic peacekeeping troops, who were deployed to quell conflicts in Republic territory[.\[65\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Battle_of_Jedha-65)

The fleet consisted of several starship types, such as Emissaryclass cruisers, [Pacifier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pacifier-class_sector_patrol_cruiser)[-class sector patrol cruisers,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pacifier-class_sector_patrol_cruiser) [Longbeam](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Longbeam-class_corvette)

[cruisers,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Longbeam-class_corvette) and [Z-28 Skywing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-28_Skywing) starfighters. Despite the existence of the RDC, the Republic was at the height of an era of peace that had lasted for centuries, with neither the Hutts nor the Mandalorians posing a threat. As a result, the Emissary-class resembled a work of art, its crenellations and spires designed to be symbolic of the Republic in a state of tranquility. The RDC operated alongside the Jedi Order as well, allowing the peacekeepers to store their personalized starfighters, the [Jedi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Vector) [Vectors,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Vector) in the hangars of its Emissary-class cruisers[.\[6\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Light_of_the_Jedi-6) The Republic rescue fleet was also maintained at the time of the High Republic[.\[73\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-HRA_2-73)

The Grand Army of the Republic was composed of clone troopers.

Towards the end of the Separatist Crisis, the Galactic Senate passed the Military Creation Act, which led to the creation of the Grand Army of the Republic and the Republic Navy.[\[8\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Rise_of_the_Separatists-8) Outfitted with [armor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_trooper_armor) weapons and gear[,\[135\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-ArmorDB-135) clone troopers were the backbone of the Republic Military. Bred for loyalty and combat, clones symbolized the future of galactic warfare at the time of the Clone Wars, the conflict that derived its name from their ranks. The training that they experienced molded the clone troopers into a highly efficient army[,\[136\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-ClonesDB-136) and because they retained the ability to think creatively despite their stunted independence[,\[9\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_II-9) they were considered far superior to their droid counterparts. In their first battle against the Separatist Droid Army, the Grand Army displayed a level of military strength unseen in the history of the galaxy[.\[136\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-ClonesDB-136)

There were several types of clone soldiers that stood as a breed apart from their rank-and-file brethren. The [Advanced Recon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Advanced_Recon_Commando) [Commandos,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Advanced_Recon_Commando) for example, were noted for their elite status and capacity for independent thinking. # **Economy[\[](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Republic?veaction=edit§ion=30) ]**

"The Republic is near bankruptcy due to the cost of this war!"

―Bail Organ[a\[145\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-HoBS-145)

The [Republic credit](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Credit_Standard) was the [currency](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Currency) of the Galactic Republic. Beyond Republic space, however, credits had no value on frontier worlds like Tatooine[.\[10\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_I-10)

The Republic possessed immense economic power,[\[146\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-CoruscantDB-146) having maintained peace—with the aid of the Jedi Knights—throughout the known galaxy for almost a millennium. Peace, in turn, promoted commerce. Under the Republic's protection, Naboo and other culturally rich worlds were able to profit from lucrative tourism[.\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)

## ![](_page_87_Picture_3.jpeg)

Corporations possessed considerable influence in galactic politics during the era of the Republic.

The business sector experienced an exponential growth in wealth and influence through widespread commercialism, with massive corporations funneling substantial amounts of capital into advertising their essential services to the wider galaxy. This unbridled "brand-building" initiative generated steady profits for vast enterprises like the Trade Federation[.\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)

The Republic's expansion into the Outer Rim Territories proved lucrative to both the galactic government and the private sector. [Eleven Star Marketing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Eleven_Star_Marketing), a subsidiary of the Trade Federation, worked closely with the [Republic Ministry of](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Ministry_of_Economic_Development) [Economic Development](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Ministry_of_Economic_Development) to [propagandize](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Settle_the_Outer_Rim) the exploration and colonization of the Outer Rim, convincing the lower class citizens of the Inner Rim to see the galactic frontier as an opportunity for profit. This arrangement would entangle Republic politicians with corporate lobbyists, both of which profited from the flow of settlers who had to pay tariffs and registration fees in order to reach the Outer Rim through one of the Federation's hyperspace routes[.\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)

## ![](_page_88_Picture_3.jpeg)

The Republic filled its war chest by encouraging its citizens to buy war bonds.

The Separatist Crisis had a significant impact on the Republic economy, with major worlds, like Serenno, Raxus, and Onderon seceding from the pan-galactic union. As a result, the Republic lost access to the resources and tax revenue of the Separatist worlds. Diminished taxation revenue only compounded the Republic's debt, throwing the Senate into a crisis that ultimately led to open war between the Republic and the Separatist

## Alliance[.\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)

The Clone Wars disrupted the galactic economy as the Senate exhausted its options to fund the Grand Army of the Republic while staving off bankruptcy[.\[145\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-HoBS-145) The government urged its people to buy [war bonds,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/War_bond) using [propaganda](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Buy_Republic_War_Bonds) to convince the citizenry that it was their patriotic duty to give financial support to the war effort. In addition, the Republic sought to keep its war chest filled by imposing higher taxes on civilian skylanes, however unprecedented it was on cosmopolitan worlds like Coruscant[.\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)

The Senate also weighed the need for additional clone troopers against the corresponding cost of growing supplemental units to replenish the Grand Army's ranks[.\[145\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-HoBS-145) Funding the military had all but bankrupted the Republic treasury, which, in turn, shut down some of the basic services that the Republic had always provided for civilians who lived in the lower levels of Coruscant[.\[147\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Pursuit_of_Peace-147)

## ![](_page_89_Picture_5.jpeg)

The Senate voted in favor of deregulating the banks in order to continue financing the Republic war effort.

The Republic imposed regulations on the banking industry prior to the Clone Wars, but the war's increasing cost led the Senate to consider deregulating the banks in order to access new lines of credit. After much debate, the Senate passed the [Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_financial_reform_bill) [financial reform bill.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_financial_reform_bill) The banks were deregulated, and the Republic submitted applications for further loans to support the production of more clone units[.\[145\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-HoBS-145)

Following the Republic [invasion of Scipio,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Invasion_of_Scipio) the [Inter Galactic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Inter Galactic_Banking_Clan) [Banking Clan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Inter Galactic_Banking_Clan) surrendered its financial assets to the office of Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine. As a result, the Republic gained direct control over one of the galaxy's most influential commerce guilds[.\[148\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-IGBC-DB-148)

Though the Clone Wars had exacted a heavy toll on the Republic treasury[,\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15) the Republic remained a powerful economic force in the galaxy[.\[146\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-CoruscantDB-146) The Separatist Alliance, while supported by the resources of corporate barons[,\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15) was eventually overwhelmed by the economic and military power of the Republic[.\[146\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-CoruscantDB-146)

## **Science and technology[**

"You know how hard it is to find all original parts from way back in the Galactic Republic?"

―Peli Motto, to Din Djari[n\[149\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Chapter_5-149)

The Galactic Republic invested in aesthetics and technological advancements for the starship industry. During the Republic's reign, starships were designed to be aesthetically pleasing in order to meet the customers' demand for "style and elegance" in their vessels. Craftsmanship, however, gave way to practicality as the Republic transitioned into the Galactic Empire, leading to the decline of sleek lines in favor of harsh uniformity, mass production, and sheer size[.\[25\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-TSWB-25)

## ![](_page_91_Picture_5.jpeg)

Millions of identical clones were bred to fight as soldiers of the Republic.

Cloning was a science in which an organism was "genetically duplicated into one or more identical copies." While neither the Senate nor the Jedi had any knowledge of the clone army's development prior to the Clone Wars, the Republic became heavily invested in the field of genetics throughout its conflict with the Separatists. The Kaminoans used their skills to transform the science of cloning into big business, resulting in the mass production of clone troopers who numbered in the millions. Their expertise all but perfected the art of genetic duplication. However, cloning [humans](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human) was still a flawed science that occasionally produced aberrations. Nevertheless, the Kaminoans' cloning methods made it possible to alter clones at the genetic level, making them more docile and age twice as fast as natural-born humans[.\[25\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-TSWB-25) They were also just as capable of engineering a clone for the purpose of pure genetic replication, giving it neither growth acceleration nor behavioral modification for greater docility[.\[9\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_II-9)

## ![](_page_92_Picture_3.jpeg)

The Clone Wars drove the Republic's technological advancement.

The Clone Wars gave the Republic an incentive to spearhead technological innovation in spacecraft and terrestrial transportation for military purposes. The result was a legacy with far-reaching effects in the industry of science and technology. The Star Destroyer, the most visible representative of modern military [capital ships,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Capital_ship) originated with the development of the Republic Navy's Venator-class Star Destroyer. The [X-wing starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X-wing_starfighter) evolved from the [Z-95](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-95_Headhunter) [Headhunter;](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Z-95_Headhunter) the [BTL Y-wing starfighter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BTL_Y-wing_starfighter) was developed during the Clone Wars, and was still considered relevant long after the Republic's time. The Republic developed all terrain [walkers,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Walker) including the six[-legged](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Limb) [All Terrain Tactical Enforcer,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/All_Terrain_Tactical_Enforcer) as a response to the Separatist [Armored Assault Tank](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Armored_Assault_Tank_Mk_I) and other droid battle tanks. This in turn influenced the developmental design of far larger vehicles, such as the Imperial [All Terrain](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/All_Terrain_Armored_Transport) [Armored Transport](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/All_Terrain_Armored_Transport) and the First Order [All Terrain Mega Caliber](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/All_Terrain_Mega Caliber_Six) [Six,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/All_Terrain_Mega Caliber_Six) than what the Republic fielded in the Clone Wars.[\[25\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-TSWB-25)

## ![](_page_93_Picture_3.jpeg)

The electro-proton bomb was capable of destroying an army of droids in a single attack.

The Republic employed brilliant [scientists,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scientist) such as [Doctor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Doctor) [Sionver Boll](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sionver_Boll)[\[150\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Sionver_Boll_DB-150) and Doctor [Nala Se,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nala_Se) [\[151\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Nala_Se_DB-151) who contributed their expertise to the war effort against the Separatists[.\[87\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-The_Zillo_Beast-87)[\[152\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Conspiracy-152) Boll developed the electro-proton bomb[,\[150\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Sionver_Boll_DB-150) an experimental explosive device capable of generating a massive burst of [electromagnetic pulse](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Electromagnetic_pulse) energy to disable battle droids and any other form of technology[.\[87\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-The_Zillo_Beast-87) Nala Se was the foremost scientist in the field of cloning; she supervised the development of the Republic's clone army[,\[151\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Nala_Se_DB-151) although Boll was also familiar with cloning as Chancellor Palpatine ordered her to clone a dead Zillo Beast[.\[91\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-The_Zillo_Beast_Strikes_Back-91)

## **Society and culture[**

**Civilization[\[](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Republic?veaction=edit§ion=33) ]**

"Tales of its founding have been occluded by the haze of intervening millennia, and its rebirth a thousand years ago was regarded as the latest evolution in an unending cycle. Like the greatest of trees, however, the Republic's decline began from within, with a deep rot that was not apparent until it was too late."

―Janyo[r\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)

## ![](_page_96_Picture_5.jpeg)

The Core Worlds represented the wealthiest and most prestigious region in the Galactic Republic.

The Galactic Republic governed the galaxy as a democratic union[,\[1\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-RepublicDB-1) and as such, it featured a diverse society of humans and various alien [species.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Species) [\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15) Coruscant in particular was emblematic of Republic society with its diverse mix of citizens and culture, making the Republic capital the "vibrant heart" of the galaxy[.\[146\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-CoruscantDB-146) Overall, being a galactic government, trillions lived under the Republic, with every one of those beings an individual who had their own thoughts and opinions on current issues; as such, complete consensus was impossible, yet successful Chancellors, like Lina Soh, nonetheless proved it was possible to bring about effective, galactic rule. In Soh's case, she argued to her people the advantage of coming together to make life better for all. Others failed to bring about that same unity by making impossible promises, threats, or abusing power[.\[6\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Light_of_the_Jedi-6)

The Core Worlds, where Coruscant was located, was home to the most developed systems in the Republic. Various Core planets served as capitals for galactic governments such as the Old Republic, the Empire, and the New Republic. The prosperity of the Core made its worlds the wealthiest and most prestigious members of the Republic, though it was also a source of tension between the Core and the more distant regions of the galaxy. People who lived outside of the Core generally viewed "Core Worlders" as arrogant, entitled, and apathetic to the well-being and needs of other regions. A common criticism of Republic politics was that the system disproportionately favored the Core Worlds to the point of ignoring the rest of the galaxy.[\[25\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-TSWB-25)

## ![](_page_97_Picture_4.jpeg)

## ![](_page_98_Picture_2.jpeg)

The Republic capital's lower levels were ridden with crime and poverty.

Coruscant was perceived as perpetuating the Core Worlds' reputation for extreme wealth and privilege. Notwithstanding its unique status as the galactic capital, the whole planet was covered by a city of levels built upon levels throughout the ages of galactic history. The tallest skyscrapers rose over fivethousand levels above the planet's natural surface, and only those who could afford to live in the upper city had access to the comfortable and luxurious lifestyle for which Coruscant was famous. The disparity was such that the lower levels were notorious for widespread crime, poverty, and being devoid of natural sunlight[.\[25\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-TSWB-25)

Republic architecture was an architectural style that demonstrated sweeping lines. This particular design was prominently featured on the city-covered planet of Coruscant, although by the time of the Clone Wars, the Republic gradually adopted a brutalist or bold form of architecture. Introduced by military architects such as Orson Krennic, it ultimately supplanted the Republic style as the dominant architectural form of the Galactic Empire[.\[154\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Visual_Guide-154)

[Galactic Basic Standard](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Basic_Standard) was the most prevalent [language](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Language) in the

Republic[,\[155\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Aliens_of_the_Galaxy-155) spoken primarily by human[s\[156\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-sweWI-156) and the [Pantorans.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pantoran) [\[157\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-sweTA-157) A heavily accented dialect of Basic, with many differences in grammar and vocabulary, was spoken by the Gungans of Naboo[.\[10\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_I-10) The translation of the Sith language, known as [ur-Kittât,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ur-Kitt%C3%A2t) was forbidden under Galactic Republic law. [Protocol droids](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Protocol_droid) were consequently programmed against translating the language of the Sith. Darth Sidious believed that the Republic outlawed ur-Kittât out of fear of the Sith Order and its knowledge[.\[158\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Secrets_of_the_Sith-158)

## ![](_page_99_Picture_3.jpeg)

Republic citizens turned away from galactic politics to follow escapist pursuits.

The peaceful age of the Republic allowed the people of the galaxy to pursue leisure activities, some of which achieved galactic-level attention. The [Boonta Eve Classic,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Boonta_Eve_Classic) while based on the independent Outer Rim world of Tatooine, beyond the Republic frontier, received coverage and attention as far as the Core Worlds. Prior to the Clone Wars, the Republic's twilight years became an era of escapism as citizens of all classes sought entertaining distractions from their daily lives.

Professional podrace circuits and other circuses diverted the people's attention from the growing corruption that permeated the Galactic Senate. Nevertheless, the general public's fascination with various forms of entertainment was driven by an innate desire for stories about good versus evil and situations of escalating danger[.\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)

## ![](_page_100_Picture_3.jpeg)

The Jedi Order eschewed prestige and celebrity, making them mysterious to the Republic they served.

Whereas the Republic evolved through the centuries of its existence, the Jedi Order remained rooted in its ancient traditions, effectively putting the [Jedi Code](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Code) at odds with a galactic society that strongly favored the future over the past. The inhabitants of the Core Worlds became fixated on notions of fame and fashion, and had little interest in Jedi principles like faith and tradition. The Order maintained its place in the Republic by relying on historic precedent, serving as keepers of peace and justice. Unlike corporate institutions that marketed their services to the greater galaxy, the Order had little desire to promote itself through image-building. Aside from those who witnessed the Jedi's heroic[s\[159\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Tipping_Points-159) and were awed by their [Force](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_power) [powers,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_power) the Order and its ways were forever mysterious to the majority of the Republic citizenry. The Jedi were seen by the public as an order of mystics at best and, at worst, regarded as a zealous sect of warmongers[.\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)

## ![](_page_101_Picture_3.jpeg)

A variant of the Republic crest was used during the Clone Wars.

As a result of territorial expansion, the Republic developed a sense of manifest destiny. This cultural belief was cultivated by Republic propagandists who sought to foster civic pride among the populace so that they would ultimately feel duty-bound to support the Republic's expansion from rim to rim. There was an exponential growth in Republic patriotism due to the Clone Wars and the accompanying propaganda that promoted loyalty to the state. Patriotic messages stirred an entire generation into action; however, they could not enlist in the Grand Army as its ranks were exclusive to clone troopers. Nevertheless, Republic citizens felt obliged to do their part by purchasing war bonds, reporting acts of treason, and making sacrifices to support the war effort[.\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15) The [Republic crest,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_crest) the symbol of a united galaxy under the Force[,\[160\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Complete_Vehicles_2018-160) was emblazoned on military war machines and heraldry, featuring more prominently than before.[\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)

Although the Republic expanded into the Outer Rim Territories, the region was too underdeveloped to have the same stability as the Core Worlds. As the galaxy's largest region, the Outer Rim featured a myriad of diversity through its various planets and species. The Republic government had little oversight over the Outer Rim due to the region's distance from the Core, and as such the Outer Rim remained known for its lawless, sparsely populated, and primitive worlds. Republic law was virtually nonexistent in the Outer Rim; as a result, bounty hunters, outlaws and smugglers were able to operate with impunity,[\[25\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-TSWB-25) and [slavery](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Slavery) was still practiced in spite of the Republic's anti-slave laws[.\[10\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_I-10)

## **Xenophobia[\[](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Republic?veaction=edit§ion=34) ]**

"Under Gunray's tenure, the Trade Federation would loudly counter such claims as common anti-Neimoidian prejudice."

―Janyo[r\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)

## ![](_page_103_Picture_3.jpeg)

A popular stereotype of the Neimoidian race was the fat plutocrat who embodied greed and treachery.

During the High Republic Era, Chancellor Soh wanted to promote unity amongst the various species that made up the Republic, using the phrase "We are all the Republic" to let the population know they were all part of the system;[\[6\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Light_of_the_Jedi-6) however, [xenophobia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xenophobia) was an issue in a society as diverse as the Republic. Although the Republic officially promoted unity among its member world[s\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15) and outlawed the enslavement of [sentient](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sentience) species[,\[10\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_I-10) some people were subjected to racial stereotypes.

[Neimoidians,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Neimoidian) for example, were considered obese, duplicitous and avaricious by some, including those who harbored anticorporate views. This perception of their race was a product of Neimoidian culture, which cultivated a "survival of the fittest" mentality in Neimoidian [younglings.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Youngling) [\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)

The Trade Federation used xenophobic sentiments as a means to silence its critics, insinuating that those who sought to [curtail](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Taxation_Without_Federation) [the Federation's massive influence](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Taxation_Without_Federation) were prejudiced towards its Neimoidian leaders. The [Muuns](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Muun) likewise had an image problem due to their monopoly in the Inter Galactic Banking Clan. In general Muuns saw little need to build a relationship of stability and trust with the galaxy; they simply assumed that their place in society was to continue dominating galactic finances. Their cultural standards informed their business practices; therefore, Muun-sponsored advertisements were viewed as "distant and disconnected.["\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)

During the Clone Wars, anti-alien prejudice grew as a result of the non-human races that constituted the Confederacy of Independent Systems. Vice Chancellor Mas Amedda sought to promote the Republic's multicultural aspect, having taken notice of the attention given to human citizens by the Republic media. Rather than reporting on Amedda's effort to showcase Republic diversity, [Holo Net News](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Holo Net_News) outlets focused on the Vice Chancellor's self-inclusion as the sole [Chagrian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chagrian) in an assembly of various aliens. The populace decried Amedda's [message of](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Strength_and_Unity) [strength and unity](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Strength_and_Unity), and considered him an egocentric narcissist. ## ![](_page_105_Picture_2.jpeg)

The Clone Wars fueled the Republic population's anti-droid sentiment.

The Clone Wars also inflamed the population's [anti-droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anti-droid_sentiment) [sentiment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anti-droid_sentiment) to new levels due in no small part to the Separatists' reliance on battle droids. Droids had long become commonplace in galactic society, and though machines had always been the property of organics, the war cemented the view that droids were an existential threat to biological life. The Republic capitalized on this sentiment, using propaganda to illustrate droids as ruthless and dangerous tools of destruction. While the the concept of "droid terror" was embodied by battlefield machines, such as the [B1-series battle droid,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B1-series_battle_droid) [B2](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B2-series_super_battle_droid) [series super battle droid,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/B2-series_super_battle_droid) and the [droideka,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droideka) administrative [aide](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aide) droids were viewed as a different kind of "droid threat" to Republic security. There was a growing fear among the Core's affluent population that the personal assistant droids of businessbeings and government administrators could be co-

opted through spyware and used for Separatist [espionage.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Spy) [\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)

Despite the Jedi Order's millennia of service to the Republic,[\[25\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-TSWB-25) there was widespread [anti-Jedi sentiment](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anti-Jedi_sentiment) as a result of the Clone Wars[.\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15) The Sith plotted to undermine the relationship between the Republic citizenry and their Jedi guardians,[\[142\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Age_of_Republic-142) using the Clone Wars to shake the Republic's faith in the Order. [\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15) The Order fought hard to bring a swift and decisive end to the conflict[,\[2\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_III-2) but many citizens believed that the Jedi were warmongers. This perception was able to grow due in part to the Jedi's reluctance to accept accolades or embrace the fame of their status. It was the Order's preference to serve but not engage the populace; as such, the population's anti-Jedi views were the result of the Order's cultural absence as opposed to an actual refutation of their ways[.\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)

## ![](_page_107_Picture_2.jpeg)

Coruscant, the capital of the Republic, was located in the heart of the Core Worlds.

With Coruscant—birthplace of the human specie[s\[161\]—](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Uprising-161)as its capital[,\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15) the expansion of the Galactic Republic occurred along pioneering [hyperspace routes.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyperspace_route) Survey teams established navigational beacons in outlying territories such as the [Tertiary](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tertiary_Usaita_system) [Usaita system,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tertiary_Usaita_system) [\[162\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Weapon_of_a_Jedi-162) allowing colonists from the Core Worlds to venture outward towards the Outer Rim in search of opportunity and a better life[.\[163\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Smuggler)

The Galactic Republic existed across the known galaxy, stretching from the Core Worlds to the Outer Rim Territories. The Core Worlds was the heartland of the Republic, home to cosmopolitan worlds such as Coruscan[t\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15) and [Hosnian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hosnian_Prime) [Prime](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hosnian_Prime)[\[164\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Hosnian_Prime_DB-164) as well as Alderaan, one of the oldest and most prestigious planets in the Republic[.\[165\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Alderaan_DB-165) Over time, Republic expansionism led to the annexation of new member worlds.[\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15) The planet Naboo, located in the Mid Rim, was annexed by the Republi[c\[88\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Crisis_on_Naboo-88) in [867 BBY.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/867_BBY) [\[166\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Crisis_date-166)

## ![](_page_108_Picture_2.jpeg)

The Galactic Republic was a democratic union that spanned across the known galaxy for a thousand years.

During the High Republic Era, during which time the Republic actively sought out new hyperspace lane[s\[64\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Cataclysm-64) and new member worlds in the Outer Rim[,\[6\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Light_of_the_Jedi-6) Chancellor Orlen Mollo reflected on the positive paradox that was Republic expansion; with each new hyperlane, the Republic grew while the galactic community became smaller. As such, it was easier for the galaxy's planets to interact with each other. Beyond prospecting new hyperspace routes, the Republic was also actively working to expand communication[s\[64\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Cataclysm-64) into a functioning network in the Rim[,\[58\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Blade_1-58) attempting to further alliances, and hoping to shut down brewing conflicts[.\[64\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Cataclysm-64) The Republic exerted most of its power from the Core Worlds to the borders of the Mid Rim, to the extent that corporations conducted their legally dubious practices in regions where the Republic held little sway[\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15) despite the work done to bring those worlds into the galactic community during the High Republic Era[.\[6\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Light_of_the_Jedi-6)

## ![](_page_109_Picture_3.jpeg)

The Republic's colonization of the Outer Rim Territories was motivated by overpopulation, poverty, and a sense of manifest destiny.

Faced with issues like overpopulation and poverty among the lower classes, the Republic turned its attention to the Outer Rim, adopting a policy of expansion toward the uncharted territories of the known galaxy's farthest frontier. Those who inhabited the congested worlds of the galactic core were beckoned into deep space by political and corporate sponsored messages of life filled with adventure and mystery.[\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15) Such a call to new horizons could be seen during the High Republic Era, where brave citizens and families of the overpopulated Core and Colonies worlds moved to the Outer Rim through the few well-mapped hyperspace routes into the Rim. Still, the Outer Rim in specific could be home to dangers like the Nihil who took advantage of the lack of Republic law, with the Nihil waging war with the Republic for daring to enter their homelands.[\[6\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Light_of_the_Jedi-6) Into the late Republic Era, the Outer Rim remained a region where the Republic held little authorit[y\[10\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Episode_I-10) despite the work done in the High Republic period[.\[6\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Light_of_the_Jedi-6)

## ![](_page_110_Picture_3.jpeg)

The Republic's dominion extended beyond the boundaries of the galaxy, reaching as far as the extragalactic world of Kamino.

Although the Republic opposed secession, it effectively lost control over the territories that formed the Separatist Alliance. Its territorial extent changed numerous times over the course of the Clone Wars, with worlds lost and reclaimed by the Grand Army of the Republic. However, the Republic also gained new territory as a result of the war. Geonosis, the planet where the Separatist Droid Army was secretly constructed, existed beyond the Republic's borders[.\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15) The Republic Military [occupied](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Occupation_of_Geonosis) the

[Geonosian](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Geonosian) homeworld after the First Battle of Geonosis, and subsequently returned to [reconquer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Battle_of_Geonosis) the planet after it fell back under Separatist control[.\[8\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-Rise_of_the_Separatists-8) The [Kaminoan government](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ruling_Council_(Kamino)) formally joined the Republic; as such, Kamin[o\[15\]—](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)an [extragalactic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Extragalactic) plane[t\[82\]—](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SW:CL-82)was accorded full representation in the Galactic Senate, having supplied the Republic with its clone industry.[\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGalactic_Republic#cite_note-SWP-15)
