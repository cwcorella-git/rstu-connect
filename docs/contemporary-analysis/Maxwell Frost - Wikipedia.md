**Maxwell Alejandro Frost** (born January 17, 1997\)\[1\] is an American politician and activist serving as the U. S. representative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U. S._representative) for [Florida's 10th](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida%27s_10th_congressional_district) [congressional district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida%27s_10th_congressional_district) since 2023. A member of the [Democratic Party,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Party_(United_States)) he was previously the national organizing director for [March for Our Lives.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_for_Our_Lives_Action_Fund) Elected at age 25[,\[2\] Frost is the youngest member and the first [Generation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generation_Z) [Z](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generation_Z) member of the [United States Congress.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Congress)

## **Early life**

Frost was born on January 17, 1997, to a Puerto Rican mother of Lebanese descent and a Haitian father.\[3\]\[4\] His biological mother had several children.\[5\] He was adopted from birth; his adoptive mother is a special](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_education) [education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_education) teacher who migrated to the United States from Cuba in the [Freedom Flights,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_Flights) and his adoptive father is a musician from Kansas[.\[6\] He reconnected with his birth mother in June 2021.\[6\]\[7\]

Frost attended Osceola County School for the Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osceola_County_School_for_the_Arts) in [Kissimmee, Florida.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kissimmee,_Florida) [\[8\] In high school, he was part of the Technology Student Association.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technology_Student_Association) He attended [Valencia College,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valencia_College) but did not graduate[.\[6\]\[9\] For nearly a decade, Frost participated in the cadet program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Air_Patrol#Cadet_members) of the [Civil Air Patrol,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Air_Patrol) attaining the rank of [cadet major.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadet_Major)[\[10\]

## **Early career**

Frost has been organizing since around 2012, when he was active with Barack Obama's 2012 presidential campaign.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barack_Obama_2012_presidential_campaign)[\[11\]\[8\] He also volunteered with the Newtown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newtown,_Connecticut) Action Alliance, an organization created in response to the [Sandy Hook Elementary School shooting.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandy_Hook_Elementary_School_shooting)[\[6\] He has identified Occupy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupy_Wall_Street) [Wall Street,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupy_Wall_Street) the [Columbine High School massacre,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbine_High_School_massacre) the [killing of Trayvon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Trayvon_Martin) [Martin,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_of_Trayvon_Martin) and the [Orlando nightclub shooting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_nightclub_shooting) as events that affected his thinking.[\[12\] He later volunteered for Bernie Sanders,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernie_Sanders) [Hillary Clinton,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hillary_Clinton) and [Margaret Good.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Good)[\[7\]

Frost survived an incident of gun violence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gun_violence) at a [Halloween](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halloween) event in [Downtown Orlando](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downtown_Orlando) in 2016[.\[6\]\[13\]

Frost was an organizer with the American Civil Liberties Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Civil_Liberties_Union) and worked to support [Florida's 2018 Amendment 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Florida_Amendment_4) and to pressure [Joe Biden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Biden) to stop supporting the [Hyde Amendment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyde_Amendment) in 2019[.\[6\] He was the national organizing director for March for Our Lives.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_for_Our_Lives)[\[6\]\[14\] In November 2021, Frost was arrested at a voting rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voting_rights_in_the_United_States) rally in [Lafayette Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lafayette_Square,_Washington,_D. C.) led by [William](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Barber_II) [Barber II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Barber_II) and [Ben Jealous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Jealous)[.\[15\]

## **Political positions**

### **Environment**

Frost supports a Green New Deal.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_New_Deal)[\[6\] He has identified environmental justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_justice) as a priority of his campaign.[\[4\]\[7\]

## **Guns**

Frost is a strong advocate for gun control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gun_control)[.\[4\]\[7\]

In January 2023, Frost and Representative Jared Moskowitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jared_Moskowitz) sent House speaker [Kevin Mc Carthy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Mc Carthy) a letter asking him to convene a classified meeting to address mass shootings. The letter called for the FBI and other law enforcement agencies to conduct the meeting.[\[27\]

### **Healthcare**

Frost supports single-payer healthcare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single-payer_healthcare) and investing in [pandemic prevention.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pandemic_prevention)[\[4\]\[6\] \[7\] He introduced with Representative Doris Matsui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doris_Matsui) in 2024 the EPIPEN Act, which seeks to cap out-of-pocket costs for [epinephrine autoinjectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epinephrine_autoinjector) used for severe allergic reactions at \$60 per two-pack for individuals with health insurance. [\[28\]


Frost, President [Joe Biden,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Biden) and Vice President [Kamala Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamala_Harris) arrive to the Rose Garden for an announcement on the first-ever White House Office of Gun Violence Prevention in September 2023

#### **Criminal justice**

Frost wants to "build toward a future without prison".\[6\] He supports the decriminalization of sex work](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decriminalization_of_sex_work) and the [decriminalization of cannabis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decriminalization_of_cannabis) use.[\[7\]\[29\]

### **Housing**

Frost is a proponent of affordable housing. He supported Kamala Harris'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamala_Harris)s plan to build 3 million new housing units in her first term and believed that increasing housing affordability and accessibility should be a policy priority in a Harris administration. [\[30\] He was endorsed by the Florida chapter of YIMBY Action,\[31\] a [YIMBY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YIMBY) group.

Frost co-sponsored the End Junk Fees for Renters Act in 2023, which aims to eliminate extra fees imposed by landlords, ban application fees, and increase transparency in rental agreements.\[32\]

#### **Iran**

Frost believes that the 2015 Iran nuclear deal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Comprehensive_Plan_of_Action) (JCPOA) "certainly fell short in ways, but it blocked Iran's ability to build a nuclear weapon, which was an important success".[\[33\] In 2022, he supported restoration of the JCPOA but stressed "we must make it longer, stronger, and broader to cover not just the issue of nuclear weapons, but also the full range of destabilizing and threatening actions Iran engages in, like Iran's ballistic missile program and the country's support for terrorist proxies like Hezbollah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hezbollah) and [Hamas.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamas)["\[33\]

#### **Israel–Palestine**

Frost supports a two-state solution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-state_solution) to the [Israeli–Palestinian con](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli%E2%80%93Palestinian_conflict)flict and has indicated his intent to travel to Israel to promote "US leadership in bringing peace to a region that so desperately needs and deserves it".[\[34\]\[35\] He has called himself pro-Israel and pro-Palestinian.\[34\]\[35\]\[36\] In 2022, he supported unconditional U. S. military aid to Israel.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel%E2%80%93United_States_military_relations#Military_aid_and_procurement)[\[34\]\[35\]\[37\] He has criticized the Palestinian Authority's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_National_Authority) [martyr's fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_Authority_Martyrs_Fund) that compensates the families of dead and wounded militants, likening it to a recruitment tactic of [Hamas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamas) for the purpose of committing [politically motivated violence against Israel.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_political_violence)[\[34\]\[37\] Frost opposes the Boycott,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boycott,_Divestment_and_Sanctions) [Divestment and Sanctions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boycott,_Divestment_and_Sanctions) (BDS) movement and accuses it of harboring leadership from [terrorist organizations.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrorist_organization)[\[34\]\[35\]\[37\]

Frost had formerly participated in pro-Palestine activism, signing pledges with the Florida Palestine Network (FPN) and the Palestinian Feminist Pledge, calling for support of the Boycott, Divestment, and Sanctions (BDS) movement against Israel, ending military aid to Israel, and rejecting the conflation of anti-Zionism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Zionism) with antisemitism[.\[38\] In early August 2022, the Jewish news website *Jewish Insider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Jewish_Journal_of_Greater_Los_Angeles)* published a candidate questionnaire from Frost's congressional campaign that showed a shift in Frost's foreign policy positions on Israel and Palestine.[\[37\] *Jewish Insider* characterized his responses as a reversal that distanced himself from his past while declaring an aggressive stance against the BDS movement, calling for unconditional military aid to Israel, and stating his opposition to anti-Zionism.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zionism)[\[35\] His campaign later released a position paper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Position_paper) that formalized these positions.[\[34\]\[37\]

Following the Gaza war,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_war) Frost has shown a shift in his views on Israel-Palestine. While Frost voted to provide Israel with initial support following the [October 7 Hamas-led attack on Israel,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October_7_Hamas-led_attack_on_Israel)[\[39\]\[40\] as of April 2024 he has voted against providing Israel with further aid, has accused Israel of violating international law during the war, and has stated that "the bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_bombing_of_the_Gaza_Strip) [campaign in Gaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israeli_bombing_of_the_Gaza_Strip) must stop".[\[41\]\[42\]

### **Crypto regulation**

During his campaign, Frost announced a "crypto-advisory council" that would advise him during his campaign.\[43\] He received \$8,700 in contributions from crypto entrepreneur Sam Bankman-Fried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Bankman-Fried) and Sam's brother Gabriel and nearly \$1 million in help from the Super-PAC [Protect Our Future,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protect_Our_Future) almost all of it after announcing the council.[\[44\]

## **Immigration**

Frost is opposed to building a southern border wall.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico%E2%80%93United_States_barrier)[\[45\] During a January 2024 House Committee on Oversight and](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_Committee_on_Oversight_and_Accountability) [Accountability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_Committee_on_Oversight_and_Accountability) hearing on immigration, Frost proposed removing the [Statue of Liberty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statue_of_Liberty) in response to the Republican bill H. R. 2, "Secure the Border Act".[\[46\]\[47\] In March 2025, after former Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) student [Mahmoud Khalil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detention_of_Mahmoud_Khalil) was detained by [Immigration and Customs Enforcement \(ICE\)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U. S._Immigration_and_Customs_Enforcement) and his legal status was revoked, Frost remarked that his arrest should "scare everyone.["\[48\] In April 2025, Frost traveled to El Salvador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Salvador) with three other members of Congress to advocate for the release of [Kilmar Abrego Garcia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deportation_of_Kilmar_Abrego_Garcia) and others who had been [deported](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deportation) without [due process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Due_process) by the [Trump administration.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trump_administration)[\[49\]\[50\]

## **Personal life**

Frost can speak both English and Spanish.\[17\] He is a jazz drummer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz_drummer) and plays the [timbales.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timbales) [\[8\]\[14\] His nine-member high school band Seguro Que Sí (from Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_language) 'of course') performed in the parade during [President Obama's second inauguration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_inauguration_of_Barack_Obama) in 2013[.\[8\]\[51\] He also enjoys the trading card game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trading_card_game) [Yu-Gi-Oh!,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yu-Gi-Oh!_Trading_Card_Game) having played the game since his younger years.[\[52\]

Frost is a Baptist.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baptists)[\[53\]

In December 2022, Frost said he was denied a rental apartment in Washington, D. C., due to a "really bad" credit history.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Credit_history) He said his credit rating was bad because he "ran up a lot of debt running for Congress for a year and a half".[\[54\]

Frost was among a handful of Democrats who received about \$1 million in support from former billionaire and felon Sam Bankman-Fried'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Bankman-Fried)s Protect Our Future PAC, as well as the maximum individual donation of \$2,900[.\[55\] In December 2022, the U. S. government indicted Bankman-Fried after alleging that he gave investor money to progressive political candidates, among other fraudulent crimes.\[56\] After the announcement of charges against
