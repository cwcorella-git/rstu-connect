A **Mecanum wheel** is an [omnidirectional](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/omnidirectional) wheel design for a land-based [vehicle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle) to move in any direction. It is sometimes called the **Swedish wheel** or **Ilon wheel** after its inventor, Bengt Erland Ilon (1923–2008)[,\[1\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fen.wikipedia.org%2Fwiki%2FMecanum_wheel#cite_note-patent-de-1) who conceived of the concept while working as an [engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer) with the [Swedish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden) company Mecanum AB, and [patented](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patented) it in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) on November 13, 1972[.\[2\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fen.wikipedia.org%2Fwiki%2FMecanum_wheel#cite_note-patent-us-2)
