rupi kaur is a poet, artist, and performer, as a 21-yearold university student rupi wrote, illustrated, and selfpublished her first poetry collection *milk and honey*, next came its artistic sibling *the sun and her flowers*, these collections have sold over 8 million copies and have been translated into over 40 languages.

rupi's work touches on love. loss, trauma, healing, femininity, and migration, she feels most at home when creating art or performing her poetry onstage.
