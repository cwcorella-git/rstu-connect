## **Talking Shop: Burning Up**

| Date: Sat, 06/22/2019 - 17:02 |
|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Source: https://ecology.iww.org/node/3313|
| Tags: green syndicalism, green unionism, ecosocialism, Green New Deal (GND), just transition,<br>Extinction Rebellion (XR), Climate Strike, Earth Strike, Industrial Workers of the World (IWW) |
| By staff - New Syndicalist, June 22, 2019 |
| In this episode of Talking Shop, we interview Simon Pirani, the author of |
| 'Burning Up – |
| A Global History of Fossil Fuel Consumption' |
| . Simon is a writer, historian, and researcher on energy, as well as an activist involved in<br>social and labour movements. |
| In 'Burning Up', Simon argues that, throughout the twentieth century, industrialisation,<br>urbanisation and mass consumption were the driving forces for the technological changes and<br>changes in energy consumption that created the current climate crisis. He also discusses the<br>social transformations that would be needed to build fully sustainable relationships to the<br>environment. |
| We asked Simon about what climate change looks like in the present day and near future, the<br>promise of movements like the school strikes, and the climate denialism of big business. We<br>talked about how and why unions should be mobilising their membership behind climate<br>issues, and about moving beyond the false dichotomy of jobs vs positive environmental<br>policies. |
