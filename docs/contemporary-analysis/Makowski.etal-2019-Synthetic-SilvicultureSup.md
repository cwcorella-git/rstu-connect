## Glossary

## Acropetal Upward from the base or the point of attachment.

## Allometry is the relation between the size of an organism and aspects of its physiol-

ogy and morphology.

Apical bud The bud located at the end of a branch, which is the location where shoot growth occurs.

Apical control is the inhibition of lateral branch growth by shoots above it (distal shoots). If the distal shoots are cut off to remove apical control, the lateral branch can grow larger and may bend upwards.

## Basipetal Downward toward the base or point of attachment.

## Biome a large naturally occurring commu-

nity of flora and fauna occupying a major habitat, e.g. forest or tundra.

## Chronological age is the time of existence of an organism.

Climax species are the predominant plant species in an ecosystem.

Cohort senescence is the senescence of a group of plants of similar age.

Decurrent architecture A decurrent branching habit is a plant form common for shrubs.

Ecological succession is the process of change in the species structure of an ecological community over time.

Excurrent architecture Plant form with a pronounced central stem extending to the top, resulting in an overall conical or pyramidal tree shape.

## Homeostasis is the tendency toward a relatively stable equilibrium between interdependent elements, especially as maintained by physiological processes.

Lateral bud A lateral or axillary bud is located in the axil of a leaf. Each bud has the potential to form shoots, and may be specialized in producing either vegetative shoots (stems and branches) or reproductive shoots (flowers).

## Morphology the study of the form and structure of organisms and their specific struc-

tural features.

## Morphospace A morphospace is a representation of the possible form, shape or structure of an organism. Each axis of the morphospace corresponds to a variable which describes some character of the organism. Each point in the morphospace represents an individ-

ual organism.

Over-dispersion a distribution with a higher than ex-

pected variance.

Physiological age or biological age is a measure of how well or poorly an organism is functioning relative to its chronological age.

Plant Physiology is relating to the function of organs and organ systems of plants, and how they work to respond to stimuli.

## Understory is a layer of vegetation beneath the main canopy of a forest.

Senescence or biological aging is the gradual dete-

rioration of functional characteristics of an organism.

## Shoot A young branch springing from the main stock of a plant.

## Tropism the turning of all or part of an or-

ganism in a particular direction in response to an external stimulus. Phototropism: growth of a plant in response to a light. Gravitropism: growth of a plant in response of gravity.

## Vigor is the vitality of a plant and its subse-

quent performance.
