# **Power supply compatibility guide for Fractal Design Ridge case**

Your Fractal Design Ridge case supports a maximum PSU size of **125mm × 63.5mm × 130mm** (W × H × D). After extensive research from official manufacturer specifications and technical reviews, I've compiled the exact dimensions for all requested 750W+ SFX/SFX-L power supplies.

## **Critical compatibility findings**

Among the 13 power supplies researched, **12 are fully compatible** with your Ridge case, while **1 has a critical height issue** that makes it incompatible. The Corsair SF750 (2024 model) exceeds the height limit by 1.5mm, which could prevent proper installation. [hwbusters](https://hwbusters.com/psus/corsair-sf750-atx-v3-1-psu-review/)

### **Key recommendations for your purchase**

Given your history of incorrect PSU purchases, here are the safest options with the most clearance:

#### **Maximum compatibility (30mm depth clearance):**

- Corsair SF750 (Original model NOT the 2024 version)
- Silver Stone SX700-PT or SX750-PT
- Cooler Master V750 SFX or V850 SFX

#### **Highest wattage options (using full case depth):**

- Silver Stone SX1000-LPT (1000W, exact fit at 130mm) [Anand Tech](https://www.anandtech.com/show/14556/silverstones-1000w-sfxl-psu-when-less-is-more)
- ASUS ROG Loki 1000W SFX-L (1000W, 5mm clearance)
- Corsair SF850L (850W, exact fit at 130mm) [Hardware Busters](https://hwbusters.com/psus/corsair-sf850l-sfx-l-psu-review-the-best-sfx-l-psu/)

#### **Important purchase warnings:**

- **Avoid the Corsair SF750 2024 model** (CP-9020284-NA) as its 65mm height exceeds your case limit
- When purchasing, verify the model number carefully the old and new SF750 have different dimensions
- SFX-L models provide better cooling with 120mm fans but use more case depth [Corsair](https://www.corsair.com/us/en/explorer/diy-builder/power-supply-units/power-supply-form-factors-and-sizes-explained/)
- All ASUS Loki models are actually 125mm deep, not the standard 130mm for SFX-L

## **Conclusion**

Your Fractal Design Ridge case will accommodate 12 of the 13 researched power supplies. The only incompatible unit is the 2024 Corsair SF750 due to its increased height. For maximum safety and to avoid a third incorrect purchase, consider the standard SFX models from Silver Stone or Cooler Master, which provide 30mm of depth clearance and guaranteed compatibility.
