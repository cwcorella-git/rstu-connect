# **Grand Army of the Republic**

"As my first act with this new authority, I will create a Grand Army of the Republic to counter the increasing threats of the Separatists."

―Sheev Palpatin[e\[1\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Episode_II-1)

The **Grand Army of the Republic** (**GAR**), also known as the **Great Army of the Republic**, the **Republic Army**, the **Grand Army**, the **Galactic Army**, or simply the **clone army**, was the [army](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Army) branch of the [Republic Military](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Military) throughout the duration of the [Clone Wars.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_Wars) At the [time](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Time) of the [Battle of](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_Battle_of_Geonosis) [Geonosis,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_Battle_of_Geonosis) the [Galactic Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Republic) activated hundreds of thousands of [clone troopers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_trooper) to fight the [Separatist Droid Army](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Separatist_Droid_Army) of the [Confederacy of Independent Systems.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Confederacy_of_Independent_Systems) In addition to [human](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human) [clones,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cloning) all grown from the [bounty hunter](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bounty_hunter) [Jango Fett](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jango_Fett)'s [genetic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DNA) [template,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_template) the GAR also had Jedi and other non-clone leadership as well as regular conscripted soldiers, though they formed the minority of soldiers in the Grand Army.

During the [waning years](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Era) of the Republic, [Jedi Master](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Master) [Sifo-Dyas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sifo-Dyas)

contacted the [government](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Politics) of [Kamino](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kamino) to commission the creation of a clone army, convinced that [the galaxy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_galaxy) would be consumed by war. As thousands of [star systems](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_system) joined the [Separatist movement,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Separatist_Crisis) the [Galactic Senate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Senate) invested wartime powers in [Supreme Chancellor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Chancellor) [Sheev Palpatine.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Sidious) As a result, the Grand Army of the Republic was formally established, comprising thousands of clone [soldiers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Soldier) with millions more being developed in [Kaminoan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaminoan) facilities by the start of the Clone Wars. The [Jedi Knights,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Knight) having served as [peacekeepers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Law_enforcement_agency) for a [thousand](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Era) [years,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Era) became [Jedi Generals](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_General) when the Senate called on the [Jedi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order) [Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Order) to lead the Grand Army against the enemies of the Republic.

For three [years,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Standard_year) the Grand Army fought on all fronts across a war-torn [galaxy,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galaxy) from the [campaigns](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Rim_Sieges) in the [Outer Rim](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Rim_Territories) [Territories](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Rim_Territories) to a [Separatist attack](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Coruscant) on the Republic [capital](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Capital) of [Coruscant.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant) Through the machinations of Palpatine, [revealed](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_in_Palpatine%27s_office) to the [Jedi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi) as the [Sith Lord](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Lord) [Darth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth) Sidious, who was behind the war and deceiving both sides, [the clones were ultimately forced to](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Behavioral_modification_biochip) [turn against their Jedi leaders,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Behavioral_modification_biochip) [slaughtering](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Jedi_Purge) them throughout the galaxy and [inside](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Operation:_Knightfall) the [Jedi Temple](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Palace) as directed by [Order 66.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Order_66)

After Palpatine [declared](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Proclamation_of_the_New_Order) himself [Emperor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Emperor) of the [Galactic Empire,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Empire) the Grand Army of the Republic was absorbed by the [New Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Order) and reorganized into the [Imperial Army,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Army) allowing the remaining clone [troopers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Trooper) to serve as the first generation of [stormtroopers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Stormtrooper) before being deemed financially unsupportable as there was no significant threat to the galaxy and were decommissioned, and [replaced](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Project_War-Mantle) by [conscripted soldiers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/TK_stormtrooper) instead.

## ![](_page_2_Picture_7.jpeg)

Clone troopers comprised the Grand Army of the Republic throughout the Clone Wars.

[Clone troopers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_trooper) served as [infantry](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Infantry) units in the Grand [Army](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Army) of the [Republic.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Republic) [\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Clone_Troopers-15) In addition to the rank-and-file [soldier,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Soldier) the Grand Army consisted of numerous specialists, including environmentbased units like [clone cold assault troops](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_cold_assault_trooper) and [clone SCUBA](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_SCUBA_trooper) [troopers.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_SCUBA_trooper) Other variants units like [clone ordnance specialists](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_ordnance_specialist)

and [clone trooper pilots](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_trooper_pilot) were specially trained for specific functions, such as [bomb](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bomb) diffusion and flight, respectively. [ARC](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Advanced_Recon_Commando) [troopers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Advanced_Recon_Commando) and [clone commandos](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_commando) were considered elite units in the Grand Army due to their exceptional skills and specialized training[.\[16\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Clone_Trooper_Armor-16) Occasionally, due to accelerated aging, the Republic's clone troopers were reinforced with non-clone conscript squads[.\[17\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Conscript_Squad-17)

The Grand Army of the Republic possessed various [military](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Military) vehicles to support its ground forces. The principal battle [tank](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tank) of the Grand Army was the [All Terrain Tactical Enforcer.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/All_Terrain_Tactical_Enforcer) The AT-TE's six [legs](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Limb) provided the [walker](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Walker) with increased stability, but reduced the vehicle's overall speed[.\[18\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-AT-TE-18) As mobility was a key factor in the Grand Army's effectiveness, the military was outfitted with [BARC speeders](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/BARC_speeder) in order to provide the [clones](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cloning) with increased operational range, speed, mobility, and firepower that were otherwise unavailable to foot soldiers.[\[19\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-BARC-19)

## "In my book, experience outranks everything."

―Jedi Commander [Ahsoka Tano](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ahsoka_Tano) and Clone Captain Re[x\[26\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-TCWfilm-26)

At first, [color](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Color) was used to denote the [rank](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Military_rank) of Grand Army clone [officers.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Officer) This system was intended as a benefit for non-clones on the battlefield, as well as clones whose [helmet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Armor) displays malfunctioned. It fell out of use, however, as the Jedi encouraged the clones to express their individuality. As a result, the clone army gradually adopted a new system in which they customized their [armor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_trooper_armor) with unique color schemes and markings[.\[16\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Clone_Trooper_Armor-16) Non-clones and non-Jedi could also serve as [Republic officers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_officer) in the Grand Army such as [Hodnar Borrum.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hodnar_Borrum) [\[27\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Life_Debt-27)

The soldiers in the Grand Army of the Republic were bred in the cloning facilities of Kamino.

The Grand Army of the Republic, shortened to the GAR,[\[5\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Rise-5) or referred to as the Republic Army, was originally conceived by [Jedi Master](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Master) [Sifo-Dyas,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sifo-Dyas) who had [foreseen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Force_vision) [the galaxy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_galaxy) becoming consumed in war. Despite his removal from the ranks of the [Jedi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council) [High Council,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_High_Council) [\[41\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Ultimate_Star_Wars-41) whose other members rejected his ideas, Sifo-Dyas contacted the planet [Kamino](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kamino)[\[42\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Sifo-Dyas-42) in [33 BBY.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/33_BBY) [\[43\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Timelines-43) The [Kaminoans,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kaminoan) a [species](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Species) that specialized in the science of cloning, were under the impression that Sifo-Dyas represented the interests of the council. In truth, their communication was kept secret. Unaware of this deception, the Kaminoan [cloners](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Scientist) accepted the Jedi's commission to create a clone army for the [Galactic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galaxy) Republic[.\[42\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Sifo-Dyas-42)

Returning to his Jedi duties[,\[44\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Creationof-44) Sifo-Dyas did not live to witness the completion of his army. An unwitting pawn of the [Sith,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith) Sifo-Dyas was murdered by the [Pyke Syndicate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pyke_Syndicate)[\[42\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Sifo-Dyas-42) at the behest of a man named ["Tyranus"](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dooku)[\[45\]—](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-The_Lost_One-45)actually the former Jedi Master Dooku, the fallen Jedi turned to the [dark side of the Force](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_side_of_the_Force) and eventually became [apprenticed](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_apprentice) to the [Sith Lord](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Lord) [Darth Sidious.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Sidious) [\[46\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Count_Dooku-46) Sifo-Dyas was unknowingly betrayed by his friend Dooku, who orchestrated his ## ![](_page_12_Picture_8.jpeg)

Within ten years of their creation, the first clone trooper battalions were ready to serve the Galactic Republic.

Vowing to preserve the unity of the thousand-year Republic, [Supreme Chancellor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Supreme_Chancellor) Sheev Palpatine attempted to negotiate the Separatists' return to the Republic with the aid of the [Loyalist Committee](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Loyalist_Committee) and the Jedi Council. Around the same time, the [Galactic Senate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Senate) was divided in their response to the [Separatist Crisis.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Separatist_Crisis) Some [senators](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senator) advocated for the passage of the [Military Creation Act,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Military_Creation_Act) which would enable the Republic to organize a military force to defend it. Other senators such as [Padmé Amidala](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Padm%C3%A9_Amidala) opposed the bill under the belief that it would provoke a full-scale war between the Republic and the Separatists[.\[1\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Episode_II-1)

The matter was ultimately settled when the [Jedi Knight](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_Knight) [Obi-Wan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Obi-Wan_Kenobi) [Kenobi](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Obi-Wan_Kenobi) discovered both the Kamino-produced clone army and the largest army of [battle droids](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_droid) that the Separatists constructed on the planet [Geonosis.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Geonosis) Convinced that the senators would never authorize the creation of a military on their own before the Separatists attacked the Republic, [Junior](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Junior_Representative) [Representative](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Junior_Representative) [Jar Jar Binks](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jar_Jar_Binks) called on the Senate to empower the Supreme Chancellor with the authority to end the crisis of the Separatist movemen[t\[1\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Episode_II-1) through the [Emergency Powers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emergency_Powers_Act) [Act.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emergency_Powers_Act) [\[54\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Jar_Jar_card-54) Scared of the Separatists attacking with their battle droids[,\[55\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Palpatine_biography_gallery_Image_10-55) the Senate enthusiastically supported Binks' proposal; accordingly Palpatine accepted the mandate and announced the formation of the Grand Army of the Republic.[\[1\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Episode_II-1)

At the time of its introduction, the Grand Army of the Republic represented the future of galactic warfare.

After a decade of development and training, the first generation of clone troopers was called into action. Placed under the leadership of [Grand Master](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Master) [Yoda,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yoda) the Grand Army of the Republic made its debut in the [Battle of Geonosis,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_Battle_of_Geonosis) engaging the [droid](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droid) army with the element of surprise on their side. Clone troopers fought side by side as they repelled the Battle droids from the battlefield, ultimately annihilating the entire droid army, the Grand Army claimed victory in the greatest battle of the [Clone Wars—](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_Wars)so-named for the clones who composed the Republic's armed forces[.\[1\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Episode_II-1)

Throughout the conflict, the Grand Army of the Republic was deployed across the galaxy in order to put an end to the rebellion of the Separatists. The Jedi Knights assumed direct command over the clone troopers, becoming [Jedi Generals](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_General) of the Grand Army. As the Clone Wars progressed, more clone troopers sacrificed their lives out of loyalty to the Jedi and the Supreme Chancellor[.\[15\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Clone_Troopers-15) While the clones had been engineered to serve as soldiers in the Grand Army, they were also encouraged by the Jedi to develop their own distinct identities, consequently resulting in a wave of greater individuality and creative-thinking that spread through the clone ranks. As such, special unit markings and color patterns became increasingly common within the Grand Army[.\[16\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FGrand_Army_of_the_Republic#cite_note-Clone_Trooper_Armor-16)

#### Weapons and equipment[

## ]

"Minimal destruction with blasters and droid poppers only. No rockets or detonators. Check your aim."

## —Cody[98]

## ![](_page_33_Picture_5.jpeg)

The DC-15A blaster carbine was a standard-issue weapon that clone troopers used throughout the Clone Wars.

For infantry weapons, the Grand Army of the Republic had a selection of blasters, grenades, and projectile launchers. For blasters, the army made use of the DC-17 hand blaster, [26] the DC-17m blaster rifle, [57] the DC-15A blaster carbine, the DC-15A blaster rifle, the DC-15LE blaster rifle, the DC-15x sniper rifle, [133] the Z-6 rotary blaster cannon, [111] the BT X-42 heavy flame projector, [117] the T-7 ion disruptor rifle, [57] the DC-12U beam rifle, [99][134] and the Valken-38x sniper. [57] Also in use was the 773 Firepuncher rifle, which became a favorite of Republic snipers. [135]

The M3 Bulwark Blast Shield was a type of physical shield that could be worn by a clone trooper as a defensive barrier from enemy fire. [115] Clone riot troopers stationed on Coruscant wore a type of riot shield, which they wielded alongside a baton, the TZ-97 "Zapper" Shock Baton, in their policing actions. [136]

## ![](_page_34_Picture_3.jpeg)

A type of heavy repeating blaster cannon operated by the Republic

The types of grenades used were the Class-A thermal detonator, [80] its magnetic variant, [137] and the N-20 Baradium-core thermal detonator. [138] Under the command of the Jedi, the Grand Army utilized Electro Magnetic Pulse grenades to disable droids and other machines without risking organic collateral damage. [98] For melee combat, the clones within the army uses M8 combat knives as simple melee weapons. The army utilized ascension guns, [30] mortars, [38] and missile launchers, [111] such as the RPS-6 rocket launcher and RPC-2 Rocket Launcher. [139] By the final days of the war, the E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannons was used by the Republic, [62] with another heavy repeating blaster also being used at earlier points. [115]
