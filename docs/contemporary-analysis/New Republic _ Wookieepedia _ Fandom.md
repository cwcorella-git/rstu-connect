The **New Republic** [\(4 ABY–](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/4_ABY)[34 ABY\)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/34_ABY), also known as the **New Galactic Republic** or simply the **Republic**, was a representative [democratic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Democracy) [republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Politics#Republic) that was presided over by a [Chancellor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chancellor_(New_Republic)) as its elected leader and the [New Republic Senate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Republic_Senate)

which was comprised of [senators](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senator) representing their member [worlds](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Planet) from across [the galaxy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_galaxy) under the [Charter of the New](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Charter_of_the_New_Republic) [Republic.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Charter_of_the_New_Republic) While the [ecumenopolis](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ecumenopolis) [Coruscant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Coruscant) retained its [historic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline_of_galactic_history) administrative status, the Senate shifted from one world to another on a rotating basis. However, the Republic was never able to achieve the [galactic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galaxy) scope of its predecessors.

With the [death](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Death) of [Emperor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Emperor) [Palpatine](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Darth_Sidious) at the [Battle of Endor,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Endor) the [Alliance to Restore the Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alliance_to_Restore_the_Republic) [founded](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Declaration_of_a_New_Republic) the New Republic, fulfilling its [mandate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Declaration_of_the_Rebel_Alliance) to restore the [Galactic Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Republic) and a [return](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Declaration_of_Rebellion) of freedom. The Galactic Senate convened on [Chandrila](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chandrila) and [Mon Mothma](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mon_Mothma) was elected as the first Chancellor and strove to end its [struggle.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Civil_War) After [failed peace talks](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial%E2%80%93Republic_peace_talks) with [Grand Admiral](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Admiral) [Rae Sloane,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rae_Sloane) the Senate briefly relocated to [Nakadia,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nakadia) while the [New Republic Defense Force](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Republic_Defense_Force) drove the Empire from its holdings as [Counselor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Counselor_to_the_Empire) [Gallius Rax](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gallius_Rax) on [Jakku,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jakku) led the [Imperial fleet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Navy) in a [last](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Jakku) [stand](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Jakku) against the [Republic fleet.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Republic_Defense_Fleet) In its aftermath, the Empire began to collapse, allowing [Grand Vizier](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Vizier) [Mas Amedda](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mas_Amedda) to [surrender](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Instruments_of_Surrender) and signed the [Galactic Concordance,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Concordance) officially ending the conflict and Imperial [rule.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Era)

The New Republic characterised Palpatine [regime](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Order) as having been illegal and illegitimate while the Republic turned its [attention](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Republic_Era) to reshaping galactic politics, with member worlds raising their own [planetary defense forces](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Planetary_defense_forces) as its military was partially [demilitarized,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Military_Disarmament_Act) relying on egalitarian policies to remain on friendly terms with neutral [star systems](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_system) and ex-Imperial [governors.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Governor) The New Republic would eventually move some governmental functions back to Coruscant, as the world's infrastructure was already well-attuned to handling the administrative and bureaucratic of a large galactic power.

[Imperial remnants](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Remnants) continued to persist, particularly in the [Outer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Rim_Territories)

[Rim Territories,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Rim_Territories) where activities increased as the prospect of Grand Admiral [Thrawn's](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mitth%27raw%27nuruodo) return to the galaxy loomed. Meanwhile, those Imperials who fled to the [Unknown Regions](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unknown_Regions) [regrouped](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Unknown_Regions_Imperial_remnant) as the [First Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_Order) and began a secret [military buildup](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_Order_military) against the New Republic.

Years later, the New Republic faced intense political deadlock between the [Populists,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Populists) who favoured planetary sovereignty, and the [Centrists,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Centrists) who favoured a strong central authority. As a result of the [Napkin Bombing](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Napkin_Bombing) and the [Amaxine warrior crisis,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Amaxine_warrior_crisis) the senate moved to institute the role of [First Senator,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_Senator) a position that would replace the largely ceremonial [Chancellor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chancellor) as the highest office of the New Republic. Over the course of the proceedings, the Populist candidate, [Leia Organa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leia_Skywalker_Organa_Solo) was largely ruined politically following the public exposure of her parentage by [Ransolm Casterfo.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ransolm_Casterfo) After her fall from political grace, the new Populist Candidate, [Tai-Lin Garr](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tai-Lin_Garr) was [assassinated.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tai-Lin_Garr%27s_assassination) Though innocent, Casterfo was framed for the assassination by operatives of the First Order. In [29 ABY,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/29_ABY) the Centrists seceded from the New Republic, becoming the public face of the otherwise secret First Order.

During this time, [Senator](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Senator) [Leia Organa](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Leia_Skywalker_Organa_Solo) formed her own private military force, the [Resistance,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Resistance) due to the Senate's unwillingness to take action against the First Order and risk provoking another war. Despite this effort, the First Order [destroyed](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hosnian_Cataclysm) its seat of government on [Hosnian Prime,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hosnian_Prime) causing the New Republic to virtually cease to exist or function, leading to [open galactic war.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_Order-Resistance_War) The Resistance was eventually able to deal a decisive blow to the First Order at the [Battle of Exegol](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Exegol) after rallying together a [Citizens' Fleet.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Citizens%27_Fleet) A galaxy-wide [uprising against the First Order](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Uprising_against_the_First_Order) then erupted, giving rise to the hope of the New Republic's return.

## ![](_page_6_Picture_3.jpeg)

Senator Mon Mothma and her allies formed the Delegation of 2,000 to oppose the accumulation of power by Chancellor Palpatine.

As the Clone Wars drew to a close, a group of two thousand concerned senators formed the [Delegation of 2,000.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Delegation_of_2,000) They created the [Petition of 2,000,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Petition_of_2,000) which identified the entire membership of the group, formally protested Palpatine's actions, and demanded he return the emergency powers he had claimed from the Senate following the conclusion of the war[.\[28\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-DBMT-28) However, when the Jedi discovered Palpatine's identity as a [Sith Lord,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sith_Lord) and that he was not going to give back his power, a group of Jedi attempted to [arrest](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Duel_in_Palpatine%27s_office) Palpatine. They failed following the intervention of Jedi Knight [Anakin Skywalker,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Anakin_Skywalker) who joined Sidious and became his new apprentice, Darth Vader. Sidious then issued [Order 66,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Order_66) [\[18\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Episode_III-18) which forced the [clone](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_trooper) [troopers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Clone_trooper) to execute their [Jedi Generals](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Jedi_General) via pre-installed [biochips,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Behavioral_modification_biochip) [\[29\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-InhibitorDB-29) while Vader led the [siege of the Jedi Temple.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Operation:_Knightfall) Palpatine then called together the Senate in an [emergency](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Special_session_of_Congress) [session of Congress,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Special_session_of_Congress) where he denounced the attempt to arrest him as a Jedi [rebellion.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Treason) He then [declared](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Proclamation_of_the_New_Order) that the Republic would be [reorganized](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Order) into the first [Galactic Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Empire) [\(fall of the](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fall_of_the_Old_Republic) [Old Republic\)](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Fall_of_the_Old_Republic), and proclaimed himself [Emperor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Emperor) for life with the backing of the new [Imperial Senate.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Senate) [\[18\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Episode_III-18)

## ![](_page_7_Picture_6.jpeg)

The Alliance to Restore the Republic was formed by various rebel cells that opposed the rule of the Galactic Empire and restore the Galactic Republic.

Local resistance groups emerged on a number of worlds as the Empire [tightened its grip](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Military) on the galaxy, which eventually led to the outbreak of the [Galactic Civil War.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Civil_War) [\[31\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Mustafar_date-31) In time, under the leadership of Senators [Mon Mothma](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mon_Mothma) and [Bail Organa,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bail_Prestor_Organa) these local groups were organized into [rebel cells](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rebel_cell) that gradually coalesced into the full-blown [Alliance to Restore the Republic.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alliance_to_Restore_the_Republic) These resistance fighters resolved to overthrow the Emperor and restore the Republic[.\[32\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-RebelDB-32)

In [0 BBY,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/0_BBY) [\[12\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Galactic_Atlas-12) the Emperor [disbanded](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dissolution_of_the_Imperial_Senate) the Imperial Senate, the last remnant of the Old Republic, as the Empire unveiled the planet-destroying [Death Star.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/DS-1_Orbital_Battle_Station) However, the Rebel Alliance destroyed the [battle station](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_station) at the [Battle of Based in the [Nadiri Dockyards](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nadiri_Dockyards) of the [Bormea](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bormea_sector) [sector,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bormea_sector) rumors of the project spread throughout the fleet, and [Vanguard Squadron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vanguard_Squadron) was assigned to protect the construction. The first prototype under construction was known simply as the [Starhawk](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Starhawk). Around the same time, the New Republic's [Orange](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orange_Squadron) [Squadron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Orange_Squadron) was also active, patrolling the Mid Rim and helping to turn loyalists against the remnants of the Empire. Another fighter group was [Pink Squadron,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Pink_Squadron) yet all records of their actions were either classified or redacted[.\[40\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Squadrons-40)

The Imperial fleet [battled](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Defense_of_the_Victorum)

Vanguard Squadron for control over the captured Victorum, yet Kerrill's fleet was held off. Following the Imperial [Titan](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Titan_Squadron) [Squadron](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Titan_Squadron)'s [mission to Hosnian Prime,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mission_to_Hosnian_Prime) Kerrill's forces managed to secure the sector coordinates for Project Starhawk during a [battle above Desevro.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_above_Desevro) [\[40\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Squadrons-40)

Following the vote, [Battle of Jakku](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_of_Jakku) commenced with Admiral Ackbar leading the New Republic fleet to the desolate stronghold of the Empire. The late Palpatine did not want the Empire to survive his death and tasked Rax with executing his will. As part of the Contingency plan, Rax planned to blow up Jakku itself and the two belligerent forces along with it[.\[9\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Empire)

Iden pleaded with her father to come with her, but Garrick refused, urging Iden to survive. Parting with her father on good terms, Iden managed to reach an escape pod before the ship crashed into the planet below.[\[42\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Battlefront_II-42)

The New Republic Senate was engulfed in partisanship and political turmoil at the time of the Amaxine warrior crisis.

Due to the constant bickering, the Republic was unable to enforce financial regulations and to undertake regular patrols of the galaxy's shipping lanes. This indecisiveness facilitated the resurgence of several criminal cartels including the [Nikto](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Nikto) [crime lord](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Crime_lord) [Rinnrivin Di](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rinnrivin_Di)'s [cartel,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Rinnrivin_Di%27s_cartel) which was considered to be a rival to even the greatest [Hutt](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hutt) crime organizations at their peak—their targeting of [Twi'lek](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Twi%27lek) shipments shipping in the [Gaulus sector](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gaulus_sector) that housed the planet [Ryloth](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ryloth) to—which they preyed on. Rinnrivin's criminal activities had forced Ryloth's [Emissary](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emissary) [Yendor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Yendor_Brethen) to petition the Galactic Senate for assistance. In secret the [First Order,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/First_Order) a mysterious Imperial remnant, was channeling funds into the Rinnrivin Di's cartel through a paramilitary faction called the [Amaxine warriors,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Amaxine_warriors_(New_Republic_Era)) based on a supposedly warrior culture that dated back to the [dawn](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Republic_Era) of the Galactic Republic who were believed to have left the galaxy, in search of [another.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galaxy) By the time of the New Republic, they were believed to be extinct. ## ![](_page_53_Picture_5.jpeg)

The New Republic Senate was composed of senators from various worlds across the galaxy.

The office of the chancellor would have little central authority and was not an executive position[,\[4\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Bloodline-4) and was subjected to restrictions that were designed to prevent the head of state from accruing any real power[.\[21\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-TSWB-21) The Galactic Senate instead held executive powers[,\[1\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Aftermath-1) with representatives being chosen by the will of the people[,\[38\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Lost_Stars-38) giving its member worlds more influence over galactic matters of state. While the position of chancellor was formally known as Supreme Chancellor, this title was rarely used as a result of the Mothma reforms of the [charter of Chancellor,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Charter_of_Chancellor) a document that empowered the chancellor to serve as the leader of the new Galactic Senate. To prevent further abuses of the office, Mothma gave up these emergency powers and set forth a plan to give aid to war-torn worlds and bolster the training of their planetary defense forces so that they could defend themselves. The chancellor also submitted to the will of the Senate, even in military matters such as the disarmament of the [New Republic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Republic_Defense_Fleet) [Defense Fleet.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Republic_Defense_Fleet) [\[1\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Aftermath-1)

## ![](_page_56_Picture_2.jpeg)

An election was decided where to seat the Galactic Senate on a rotating basis.

A government born out of, and in many ways defined by, the shadow of war[,\[77\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Part3-77) the New Republic secured both victory and peace with the signing of the Galactic Concordance on Chandrila. Several changes were made to the political process to ensure mistakes of the Old Republic were not repeated. The New Republic, in accordance with its humanitarian values prohibited torture as part of the concordance, however the divisive topic on the treatment of former Imperials would occupy much heated debate[.\[1\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Aftermath-1) Some wished to execute and punish those responsible for war crimes, while others were dismayed at the thought of succumbing to the barbarities of the former regime, and emulating what they had fought so hard against[.\[3\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-SWP-3)

Ultimately, the fact that billions of individuals had participated in the Empire—many of them against their wishes—cooler heads prevailed, allowing for leniency in sentences and the invitation of former Imperial governors to the New Republic.[\[3\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-SWP-3) Likewise, civilian and military personnel were offered a new life within the annals of the nascent Republic[.\[38\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Lost_Stars-38) However, despite its idealism, the New Republic never took root on a scale comparable to the Old Republic[.\[70\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-TLJ_Visual-70) Additionally, the Senate passed the Military Disarmament Act which, while maintaining the New Republic Starfleet, greatly reduced its size and capabilities[.\[1\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Aftermath-1) In order to gain the support of disaffected worlds and prevent friction that had occurred during the Old Republic and later the Empire, the New Republic government chose to not settle down on Coruscant. Instead, member worlds would host the galactic capital on a rotating basis with elections deciding where to seat the Senate[.\[7\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Visual-7)

In the opinion of General Hera Syndull[a\[77\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Part3-77) and as noted by historian Beaumont Kin, something incredibly damaging to New Republic politics was the appearance of figures who had,[\[62\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Rise_and_Fall-62) in Syndulla's words, "sat out" the war against the Empire and "waited to see who came out on top.["\[77\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Part3-77) A prime example was Senator [Hamato Xiono,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hamato_Xiono) [\[62\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Rise_and_Fall-62) an elit[e\[78\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Hamato Old Databank-78) and wealth[y\[79\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Bucket) politician who embodied those who had stayed neutral in the war. Those like Xiono seemed to believe that their neutrality had resulted in a greater moral purity than their counterparts who had served in the Rebellion. All the same, Kin would also point out the split in New Republic politics was also worsened by ex-rebels who looked on their formally-neutral counterparts with contempt. At best, this contempt was a belief that these neutral actors were opportunists, while at worst these ex-rebels thought of their fellow New Republicans as Imperial sympathizers. While Kin wanted to believe that this divide between ex-rebels and neutral figures came from their "differing interpretations of peace and hope," as he argued in The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire, he fully admitted this divide resulted in gridlock for the government, especially in regard to the Imperial Remnant[.\[62\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Rise_and_Fall-62)

## ![](_page_68_Picture_5.jpeg)

Representation in the New Republic extended to human and various non-human species alike.

In contrast to the [xenophobia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Xenophobia) of the Empire, humans and aliens were equally represented in New Republic government institutions, like the Galactic Senate. About six years before the destruction of the Hosnian system, there was a divide between Populist and Centrist worlds, which reflected the two main political factions in the Senate. Populist worlds supported greater planetary autonomy, with some Populist senators advocating that citizens vote on Senate legislation and others urging the abolition of the Senate. Meanwhile, Centrist worlds advocated a stronger central government and military and took a tough stance on law and order. Some Centrists like Lady Carise Sindian were nostalgic towards the Old Empire and supported the idea of the Centrist worlds seceding from the New Republic to join the First Order[.\[4\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Bloodline-4)

## ![](_page_69_Picture_3.jpeg)

As a society, the New Republic was diametrically opposed to the Galactic Empire.

As the successor to the Alliance to Restore the Republic, the New Republic saw itself as a democracy where free exchange of ideas and opinions were actively encouraged and protected. Government debates on proposals and policies were broadcast on [Holo Net](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Holo Net) to promote transparency and accountability. In the aftermath of the Battle of Endor, the New Republic passed legislation which directed some government expenditure for non-military usage, cleaned up polluted worlds, and compensated [alien](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alien) species that had been enslaved by the Empire[.\[38\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Lost_Stars-38) Rejecting the totalitarianism and censorship of the

Galactic Empire, the New Republic eased restrictions on the media. Following the liberation of Akiva, the newly unfettered Holo Net reported on Imperial atrocities, such as the wholesale massacres of native populations carried out by Imperial commanders and Imperial suicide attacks on entrenched New Republic positions. [41] Media were allowed to debate current affairs events like the aftermath of Battle of Jakku. [38]

The working language of the New Republic was Galactic Basic Standard,[1] and was the most used in the galaxy.[84] Aside from Basic however, several other languages were spoken freely across the Republic, including Shyriiwook,[41] Rodian,[88] and Sullustese[89]

# **Economy[\[](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Republic?veaction=edit§ion=29) ]**

"You realize I could report this coercion attempt to the New Republic?"

"And we, in turn, could report your numerous dealings with the criminal underworld, all of which violate New Republic trade sanctions."

―[Imanuel Doza](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imanuel_Doza) and [Elrik Vonreg](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Elrik_Vonreg)[\[90\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-The_High_Tower-90)

## ![](_page_71_Picture_7.jpeg)

The primary form of currency in the New Republic was the credit.

The New Republic had a galactic-wide economy based on galactic shipping lanes. While its economy was based on free trade and promoting prosperity, the New Republic did not allow the free trade of most forms of spice. However, it allowed the sale and trade of weak forms of spice that could be mined and grown on numerous worlds. The Republic's prohibition on illegal and dangerous forms of spice annoyed crime lords like Rinnrivin Di, who made a living out of trafficking illicit spice.[\[4\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Bloodline-4)

The New Republic also had a policy of not allowing organized crime syndicates to permeate the galaxy, as they once did during the [Age of the Empire.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Era) However, deadlock in the Galactic Senate prevented the Republic from fulfilling this promise. By the time of the Napkin Bombing, some locations, like the space station [Chrome Citadel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Chrome_Citadel) and the Expansion Region planet of Sibensko were regarded as crime havens where various illegal activities, including [slavery](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Slavery) and the trafficking of [Glitterstim](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Glitterstim) and weapons took place. While the Republic also had financial regulations aimed at tackling organized crime, these were sporadically enforced by the time of the Napkin Bombing.[\[4\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Bloodline-4)

## **Astrography[**

**[\]](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/New_Republic?veaction=edit§ion=30)**

"Once the dust of the Galactic Civil War settled, the New Republic was a fraction of the size of the Empire and the

## Republic that preceded it."

## —Janyor[3]

## ![](_page_73_Picture_4.jpeg)

The New Republic was located throughout the known galaxy, but was only a fraction of the size of the Galactic Republic and the Galactic Empire.

Towards the end of the Galactic Civil War, the New Republic had established themselves on the planet of Chandrila and also it's sector. [41] They also took over many Imperial worlds such as Kuat, Arkanis, [9] Andara, and Akiva. [3] By the end of war, the New Republic absorbed much of Imperial Space while parts of the Core, Colonies, and Inner Rim became part of an Imperial Remnant which remained peaceful to the New Republic. [9] However this imperial remnant would eventually collapse and it's members such as Commenor became part of the New Republic. [91] By 9 ABY the Outer Rim was said to be under the jurisdiction of the New Republic. [92]

Thirty years after the Battle of Endor, the New Republic occupied vast swaths of space stretching from the Inner Core to the Outer Rim Territories, and at that time had its rotating capital on the Core World of Hosnian Prime. [5] While a fraction the size of both the Galactic Republic and Empire, its egalitarian practices left many neighboring star systems on friendly terms with the new government.[3] Some known Republic worlds included Chandrila, Akiva, [1] Coruscant, Taris, Naboo, [7] Hevurion, Mirrin Prime, [5] Arkanis, Kuat, Sullust, and Daxam IV. [4] After the First Order's secession from the Republic, a narrow region of space existed as a neutral region of systems known as the Trans-Hydian Borderlands, in which an act of aggression within would be viewed as an overt act of war. Despite this, the First Order Navy frequently crossed the Borderlands and penetrated New Republic space, which led to incidents like the Suraz engagement. In spite of these violations of the Galactic Concordance, the New Republic refused to take action against the First Order beyond issuing formal diplomatic protests.[5]

### **Behind the scenes**[

## ]

In the new *Star Wars* canon, the New Republic first appeared in the novels Aftermath and Lost Stars by Chuck Wendig and Claudia Gray respectively. [1][38] The New Republic was originally created for Star Wars Legends where it first appeared in the [1991](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/1991) novel [Heir to the Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Heir_to_the_Empire) by [Timothy Zahn.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Timothy_Zahn) [\[93\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Heir_to_the_Empire-93) The idea of rebuilding the Galactic Republic which was mentioned as part of the [Star Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Wars) [original trilogy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Original_trilogy)'s backstory traces back from [George Lucas](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/George_Lucas)' early comments about at that time unplanned [sequel trilogy](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sequel_trilogy) in [1980,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/1980) after the release of [Star Wars](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Wars:_Episode_V_The_Empire_Strikes_Back)[:](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Wars:_Episode_V_The_Empire_Strikes_Back) [Episode V](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Wars:_Episode_V_The_Empire_Strikes_Back) [The Empire Strikes Back](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Wars:_Episode_V_The_Empire_Strikes_Back). [\[94\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-George)

In the [2014](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/2014) [canon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Canon) mobile game [Star Wars: Galactic Defense](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Wars:_Galactic_Defense), [Tarfful](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Tarfful) can be seen yelling "hnn-rowr yrroonn nng rarr"—text that first appeared in the Legends comic [Star Wars: Empire's](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Wars:_Empire%27s_End) [End](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Wars:_Empire%27s_End) and meant "Long live the New Republic," when translated from [Shyriiwook.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Shyriiwook) [\[95\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-GD-95)[\[96\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-Empire) However, Galactic Defense takes place during the time when the Galactic Republic was still standing and long before the New Republic arose[.\[95\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNew_Republic#cite_note-GD-95) As such, this article assumes that the line has a different meaning in new canon media.
