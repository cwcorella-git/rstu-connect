KC Tenants has emerged as one of the most successful tenant organizing efforts in the United States, winning concrete victories for members while building a model that tenant organizations nationwide are now replicating.

## Founding and Structure

KC Tenants was founded in 2019 by:

- **Tiana Caldwell**
- **Tara Raghuveer**
- **Brandy Granados**
- **Diane Charity**

The organization functions as a **citywide tenant union** rather than focusing on a single building or landlord.

## Core Mission

> "KC Tenants is the citywide tenant union, an organization led by a multigenerational, multiracial, anti-racist base of poor and working class tenants in Kansas City, organizing to ensure that everyone in KC has a safe, accessible, and truly affordable home."

## Organizing Philosophy

### Power From the Bottom Up

KC Tenants operates on a fundamental principle:

> "The people closest to the problem are closest to the solution."

This conviction shapes their entire approach—rather than imposing solutions from above, they develop tenant leaders within their community.

### Democratic Structure

The organization emphasizes:

- Helping members **learn their legal rights**
- Creating space to **share personal experiences**
- Supporting tenants to **define their own paths toward liberation**

### Anti-Racist Foundation

KC Tenants explicitly identifies as anti-racist, centering the perspectives and agency of economically disadvantaged community members in shaping their work.

## Key Victories

### Independence Towers Rent Strike

Perhaps KC Tenants' most famous victory was the **248-day rent strike** at Independence Towers:

- Tenants withheld rent to protest uninhabitable conditions
- The strike lasted nearly 8 months
- Victory: **$300,000 in back rent forgiven**

This strike demonstrated that tenant unions can win against corporate landlords through sustained collective action.

### Kansas City Tenants Bill of Rights

KC Tenants successfully advocated for a citywide **Tenants Bill of Rights**, establishing legal protections for renters across Kansas City.

### Eviction Moratorium Work

During the COVID-19 pandemic, KC Tenants mobilized to:

- Extend eviction moratoriums
- Provide mutual aid to members
- Prevent mass displacement

## The Raghuveer Framework

Tara Raghuveer, KC Tenants director and founder, has articulated a vision for tenant unionism's role in 21st century politics:

> "The vehicle to organize poor and working-class people in the 21st century is the tenant union. The tenant union needs to be for that group of people what the labor union was in the 20th century at its peak."

This framing positions tenant organizing not merely as housing advocacy but as a **fundamental form of working-class power building**.

## Signature Language

KC Tenants has developed memorable phrases that have spread throughout the tenant movement:

- **"Every eviction is an act of violence"**
- **"My rent is my power"**
- **"Stay dangerous. Stay united. The slumlords are on notice."**
- **"Rights are what you make and what you take"**

## National Impact

KC Tenants' success has made it a model for tenant organizing nationwide. The organization's approach—combining direct action, political advocacy, mutual aid, and leadership development—has been replicated by tenant unions across the country.

In August 2024, KC Tenants helped found the **Tenant Union Federation (TUF)**, the first national federation of tenant unions since the 1970s.

## Key Organizational Elements

Based on KC Tenants' model, effective tenant unions include:

1. **Citywide scope** — not limited to single buildings
2. **Democratic structure** — members drive decisions
3. **Direct action capacity** — willing to strike
4. **Political advocacy** — pursuing legislative change
5. **Mutual aid** — supporting members' immediate needs
6. **Leadership development** — building tenant power
7. **Anti-racist commitment** — centering affected communities
8. **Clear demands** — specific, achievable goals

## Lessons for Other Cities

KC Tenants demonstrates that tenant unions can:

- **Win concrete material improvements** for members
- **Build lasting organizational power**
- **Shift public discourse** on housing
- **Change policy** at the local level
- **Inspire national organizing**

The organization proves that even in a hostile political environment, organized tenants can exercise collective power.
