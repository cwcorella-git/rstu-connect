*KCUR — August 8, 2024*

In August 2024, tenant unions from across the country formed the **Tenant Union Federation (TUF)**—the largest campaign of tenant unionism since the National Tenants Union of the late 1970s and 80s.

## Founding Member Organizations

The federation launched with five founding unions:

| Union | Location |
|-------|----------|
| **KC Tenants** | Kansas City, Missouri |
| **Louisville Tenants Union** | Louisville, Kentucky |
| **Connecticut Tenants Union** | Connecticut |
| **Not Me We** | Chicago, Illinois |
| **Bozeman Tenants United** | Bozeman, Montana |

## Leadership

**Tara Raghuveer**, KC Tenants director and founder, now serves as TUF director.

Raghuveer was listed in Time's **Time100 Next 2024** list of advocates, recognizing her leadership in the tenant movement.

## The Strategic Vision

### Standardizing Organizing Practice

Raghuveer explained the federation's core purpose:

> "We want to get really aligned about our theory of power and our methodology for building tenant unions."

The organization identified a critical gap:

> "Today, we don't have a widespread and consistent practice of tenant organizing that meets the needs of the moment."

### Why Limit Initial Membership

The decision to cap founding membership at five unions was deliberate:

> "The decision to cap the number of unions at five came largely from an inconsistency in organizing tactics."

Rather than expanding quickly, TUF prioritized **building a standardized approach** that can then be replicated.

## National Training Program

A key initiative is the **national training program** for tenant organizers:

**Jenay Manley**, KC Tenants organizer and TUF national leadership team member, explained:

> "[The program] helps learn the nuts and bolts of organizing. Other people, other unions across the country can join even if they're not currently in the Tenant Union Federation."

The training program:

- Teaches organizing fundamentals
- Supports growing unions with limited experience
- Creates consistency in practice across the movement
- Is open to unions beyond TUF membership

## Federal Policy Focus

### Rent Regulation at the National Level

Raghuveer identified federal policy as the central priority:

> "That's a big focus of the Federation in the next several months and years... actually winning rent regulation at the federal level."

The federation will push for:

- Federal rent caps
- Tenant protections at all governmental levels
- Coordinated national advocacy

### Why Federal Focus Matters

State-by-state organizing faces significant obstacles:

- 31 states have preemption laws banning local rent control
- Corporate landlords operate across state lines
- Housing finance is federally regulated
- Only federal action can address the scale of the crisis

## Expansion Plans

The federation plans to:

- **Expand membership in 2025**
- Focus on developing training and support for new tenant organizations
- Build toward national coordinated action

## Historical Significance

The establishment of TUF marks a significant moment:

- First national tenant federation since the **National Tenants Union** (late 1970s-1980s)
- Represents a new phase of organized tenant power
- Builds on decades of local organizing into national coordination

## Implications for the Movement

### From Local to National

TUF represents the maturation of the tenant movement:

1. **Local unions** build power and win victories
2. **Successful models** (like KC Tenants) demonstrate what's possible
3. **Federation** coordinates strategy and shares resources
4. **National coordination** enables federal policy change

### The Labor Parallel

Raghuveer's vision positions tenant unionism as the successor to labor unionism:

> "The tenant union needs to be for that group of people what the labor union was in the 20th century at its peak."

Just as the AFL-CIO coordinates labor unions nationally, TUF aims to coordinate tenant unions for maximum power.

## Lessons for Local Organizers

### You Don't Have to Start From Scratch

TUF's training program means new tenant unions can:

- Learn proven organizing methods
- Avoid reinventing the wheel
- Connect with experienced organizers
- Join a national network

### Local Wins Build National Power

Every local victory strengthens the national movement:

- Demonstrates tenant organizing works
- Develops experienced organizers
- Creates models for replication
- Builds toward critical mass for federal change

### The Path Forward

1. Build local tenant unions using proven methods
2. Win concrete local victories
3. Connect with national federation
4. Coordinate for federal policy change
