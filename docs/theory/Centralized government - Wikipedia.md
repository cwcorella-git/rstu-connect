## **Centralized government**

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

A **centralized government** (also **united government**) is one in which both executive and legislative [power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_power) is concentrated centrally at the higher level as opposed to it being more distributed at various lower level governments. In a national context, centralization occurs in the transfer of power to a typically [unitary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitary_state) [sovereign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sovereign_state) [nation state.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nation_state) Executive and/or legislative power is then minimally delegated to unit subdivisions (state, county, municipal and other [local](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government) [authorities\)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_government). [Menes,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menes) an [ancient Egyptian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Egypt) [pharaoh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharaoh) of the [early](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Early_Dynastic_Period_of_Egypt) [dynastic period,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Early_Dynastic_Period_of_Egypt) is credited by classical tradition with having united [Upper and Lower Egypt,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_and_Lower_Egypt) and as the founder of the [first](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Dynasty_of_Egypt) [dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Dynasty_of_Egypt) (Dynasty I), became the first ruler to institute a centralized government[.\[1\]](#page-1-0)

All [constituted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_district) governments are, to some degree, necessarily centralized, in the sense that even a [federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation) exerts an authority or prerogative beyond that of its constituent parts. To the extent that a base unit of society – usually conceived as an individual [citizen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizenship) – vests authority in a larger unit, such as the [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_(polity)) or the local [community,](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community) authority is centralized.
