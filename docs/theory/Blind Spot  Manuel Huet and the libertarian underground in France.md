## **Blind Spot : Manuel Huet and the libertarian underground in France**

## **Date:** Unknown

**Source:** https://libcom.org/article/blind-spot-manuel-huet-and-libertarian-underground-france**Tags:** Imanol, Spanish resistance, French Resistance, anarchism, Kate Sharpley Library

Manuel 'Manolo' Huet Piera was part of the anarcho-syndicalist action groups in the years before the Spanish Revolution, fought in the Spanish Civil War and worked in the Ponzán network smuggling escapers and refugees out of Vichy France during the Second World War. After the war, Huet continued to aid the resistance to Francoism where, until now, his contribution was necessarily unacknowledged. Translated by Paul Sharkey from El Angulo Muerto : Manuel Huet y la clandestinidad libertaria en Francia (2023)
