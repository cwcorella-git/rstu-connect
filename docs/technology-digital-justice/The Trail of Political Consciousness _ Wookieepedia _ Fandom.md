# **The Trail of Political Consciousness**

## Contributors to Wookieepedia

9–11 minutes

"He's writin' a manifesto. Did he tell you? Apparently, the only thing keepin' us from liberty is a few more ideas."

―Arvel Skeen, to Cassian Ando[r\[1\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-Axe-1)

The untitled journal known to some as **The Trail of Political Consciousness**, or **Nemik's manifesto**, was written and voiced by the [rebel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Axis_network) [Karis Nemik.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Karis_Nemik) It denounced the [Galactic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Empire) [Empire](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Empire) and recounted Nemik's thoughts on Imperial oppression and the cause of rebellion throughout [the galaxy.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_galaxy) In [5 BBY,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/5_BBY) Nemik was still writing his manifesto, and searching for a fitting title, while telling such information to [Cassian Jeron Andor,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cassian_Jeron_Andor) who had recently joined Nemik's [team](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aldhani_rebel_infiltration_team) of rebels that were planning a [heist](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aldhani_heist) on the [planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Planet) [Aldhani.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aldhani) Fellow rebel [Arvel Skeen](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arvel_Skeen) was also aware of the work. Following Nemik's [death,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Death) team leader [Vel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vel_Sartha) [Sartha](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vel_Sartha) gave Andor the manifesto, as Nemik had insisted that Andor have it. After returning to the planet [Ferrix](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ferrix) following the death of his [adoptive mother,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maarva_Carassi_Andor) Andor listened to the manifesto.

Eventually, the manifesto spread throughout the galaxy, something the Empire attempted to prevent but was unable to. By [1 BBY,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/1_BBY) high-ranking members of the [Imperial Security](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Security_Bureau) [Bureau](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Imperial_Security_Bureau) were unaware of who had created it.

## Nemik's manifesto was kept on a datapad

The Trail of Political Consciousness, [\[3\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-100_Objects-3) also known as Nemik's manifesto[,\[4\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-Databank-4) was the nickname of an untitled journal recorded by [Karis Nemik,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Karis_Nemik) [\[3\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-100_Objects-3) a rebel who resented the rule of the [Galactic](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Empire) [Empire,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Empire) [\[1\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-Axe-1) to document his idealistic views. Kept in a pocketsized [datapad,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Datapad) the manifesto was voice recorded and supported by [Aurebesh](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aurebesh) text[.\[5\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-Rix_Road-5) The manifesto discussed Nemik's evolving thoughts on the cause of rebellion[.\[4\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-Databank-4) It detailed the meaning of freedom and oppression in Nemik's point of view and criticized the tyranny of the Empire[.\[5\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-Rix_Road-5) A section on [mercenaries](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mercenary) named "The Role of Mercenaries in The Galactic Struggle for Freedom" drew several conclusions; as weapons were tools, their users were assets that could be used for the advantage of anyone, and that the Empire's lack of moral boundaries allowed for insurgents to adapt[.\[6\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-Eye-6)

There will be times when the struggle seems impossible. I know this already. Alone, unsure, dwarfed by the scale of the enemy. Remember this. Freedom is a pure idea. It occurs spontaneously and without instruction. Random acts of insurrection are occurring constantly throughout the galaxy. There are whole armies, battalions that have no idea that they've already enlisted in the cause. Remember that the frontier of the Rebellion is everywhere. And even the smallest act of insurrection pushes our lines forward. And then remember this. The Imperial need for control is so desperate because it is so unnatural. Tyranny requires constant effort. It breaks, it leaks. Authority is brittle. Oppression is the mask of fear. Remember that. And know this, the day will come when all these skirmishes and battles, these moments of defiance will have flooded the banks of the Empire's authority and then there will be one too many. One single thing will break the siege. Remember this. Try.

## ![](_page_4_Picture_6.jpeg)

Karis Nemik talks to Cassian Andor about his manifesto.

Karis Nemik begun developing his manifest[o\[1\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-Axe-1) by [5 BBY,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/5_BBY) [\[7\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-Date-7) when he became part of a [team](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aldhani_rebel_infiltration_team) of [rebels](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Axis_network) led by the [human](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Human) [Vel](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vel_Sartha) [Sartha](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vel_Sartha) which was planning a [heist](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aldhani_heist) on the [Imperial outpost](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aldhani_dam) on the [planet](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Planet) [Aldhani.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Aldhani) One of the team, [Arvel Skeen,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Arvel_Skeen) was amused by Nemik's project and tried to listen to his companion's idealistic speaking. After [Cassian Andor,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Cassian_Jeron_Andor) operating under the name "Clem," joined the team, Skeen introduced the new member to Nemik's unfinished manifesto, joking that "a few more ideas" was keeping them from liberty. Nemik enthusiastically spoke about his views on the Empire's oppression. When Skeen asked Andor what he believed, the man stated he knew what he was against and that everything else had to wait. Upon hearing the response, Nemik labeled Andor as the ideal reader for his manifesto, speaking about the journal more before another of the team, [Taramyn Barcona,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Taramyn_Barcona) interrupted their conversation[.\[1\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-Axe-1)

As the team journeyed to the Aldhani outpost, Andor revealed, after a confrontation with Skeen, that he had been hired to join the team and that he had taken the job only for the [money.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Galactic_Credit_Standard) Nemik was surprised at that fact[,\[1\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-Axe-1) but soon came to the conclusion that hired guns could be an important part in the fight against the Empire[.\[3\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-100_Objects-3) When he could not sleep the night before their mission, he begun writing an addition "The Role of Mercenaries in The Galactic Struggle for Freedom," which was influenced by Andor. The next morning, he told Andor about the section and its conclusion. Andor agreed with Nemik that the Empire did not play by the rules, but then argued that they would not learn from or care about the insurgents, leading to a short debate between the pair[.\[6\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-Eye-6)

#### ―Vel Sartha and Cassian Ando[r\[6\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-Eye-6)

Nemik's team successfully carried out the heist and stole around eighty million credits, with the loss of Barcona and their inside man [Lieutenant](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lieutenant) [Gorn.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Gorn) Nemik himself was critically injured during the escape and taken to the [alien](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Alien) [Doctor](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Doctor) [Quadpaw](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Quadpaw) on the [moon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Moon) [Frezno,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Frezno) where the [surgeon](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Surgeon) attempted to save his life. In his last moment of consciousness, the injured rebel requested for Andor to be given his manifesto. In the meantime, however, Skeen tried to convince Andor to betray their team and take the credits for themselves, only for Andor to kill him. Just after Nemik had died in surgery, Andor confronted Sartha and announced he was leaving with his cut of the credits. Sartha handed Andor Nemik's manifesto, adding that the late rebel had insisted for him to have it. Although Andor said that he did not want it, Sartha insisted, so he reluctantly accepted it before leaving[.\[6\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-Eye-6)

[imprisoned.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Prison) [\[8\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-Announcement-8) When he eventually [escaped](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Narkina_5_prison_escape) [incarceration,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Narkina_5_Imperial_Prison_Complex) Andor returned to Niamos and recovered the box from the hotel room while new hotel guests slept there. While checking the box, he opened the manifesto, which begun playing Nemik's recorded content briefly before he closed it again to not disturb the sleeping guests. Learning shortly after that his [adoptive](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Parent) [mother,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Parent) [Maarva,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maarva_Carassi_Andor) had died[,\[2\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-Daughter-2) Andor returned to his [homeworld](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Homeworld) of [Ferrix](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ferrix) to attend her [funeral.](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maarva%27s_funeral_procession) The night he arrived, he stayed in the wreckage of his [family's](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Andor_family) [old hauler,](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Andor_family_hauler) where he listened to Nemik's manifesto while a thunderstorm raged outside.[\[5\]](about:reader?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstarwars.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FThe_Trail_of_Political_Consciousness#cite_note-Rix_Road-5)
