| This week, we speak with Lill, a resident of Whitesburg, Kentucky. Whitesburg is located in
Letcher County the proposed home to a new federal prison to be built on a mountaintop
removal site. We have previously covered the strong local organizing in Letcher County that
had helped put a stop to this toxic proposal. In light of recent efforts by the Bureau of Prisons
to put the prison back on the table, we wanted to go in depth with a local organizer to help us
understand the current situation and how others can support the struggle there. |
