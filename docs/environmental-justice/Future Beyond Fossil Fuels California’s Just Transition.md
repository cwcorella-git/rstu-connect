## **Future Beyond Fossil Fuels: California's Just Transition**

**Date:** Thu, 05/28/2020 - 18:41 **Source:** https://ecology.iww.org/node/3408**Tags:** green unionism, just transition, Richmond, Bay Area, California, No Coal in Richmond (NCIR), North America's Building Trades Unions (NABTU), United Steelworkers of America (USW), Railcon15, fossil fuel capitalism By staff - Sunrise Movement , May 1, 2020 You may have heard the term 'Just transition' floating around, but what does it mean? This webinar will focus on what a just transition means for workers in California, and how the vision of a Green New Deal can guide the much-needed economic recovery from the COVID crisis.

This video features IWW Environmental Unionism Caucus cofounder, Steve Ongerth, speaking on workers, unions, and just transition in Northern California.
