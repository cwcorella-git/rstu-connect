# **NHJA NEW Protections for Tenants!**

**AB 121: Fee Transparency Implementation Toolkit**

#### **Overview**

AB 121, eective Wednesday, October 1, 2025, will increase rental fee transparency for tenants. It sets new protections related to fee transparency for tenants in the rental process by establishing limitations on application fees, requiring a full, clear disclosure of all fees within a lease, and requiring a fee-free way for tenants to pay their rents.

### **Key Changes**

- Tenants must receive a refund of their application fee if the unit is not rented to them, outside of actual costs for background or credit checks)
- Application fees must not be charged to a minor,
- Within a tenant's lease, the total rent must be presented as a single number
- Rent cannot be charged higher than what is listed in a tenant's lease
- Landlords are able to charge tenants in their rent for utilities (water, electric, gas) that is outside of their rent total (this number must be approximated)
- Tenants must have access to a fee-free method for rental payments
- Tenants may bring a civil action for any violations related to these provisions

#### **Know your NEW Rights!**

- Application Fees
 - Fees for applications, credit checks, and background checks MUST be refunded if:
    - The unit is rented to someone else AND
    - The background check or credit check hasn't been run.
- Clear Rent Pricing
 - Your rent must be listed as ONE AMOUNT that includes all mandatory fees. Landlords CANNOT CHARGE MORE than that amount.\*
    - \*Utilities can be billed separately only if:
      - The utility company cannot directly bill you or the water is master metered (metered for the whole building, not per unit).

- There must be a clear explanation of the reason above. The landlord cannot charge more than the actual cost of the water, gas, or electric for your unit.
- Rent Payment Options
 - Landlords must provide at least one method to pay rent or fees that:
    - Does NOT include extra fees; and
    - Does NOT require your bank account info.
 - If you pay online:
    - The charge cannot be more than the online portal's actual fee.
    - This fee must be clearly listed in your rental agreement.
- Enforcement and Penalties
 - If your landlord breaks these rules, you can sue them.
 - A judge can award damages, attorney's fees, and up to \$250 for each deceptive violation.

#### **Talking Points**

- Tenants often discover additional charges after signing a lease, making it harder to aord rent. These new protections will bring transparency in rental agreements and prevent surprise costs.
- Renters deserve to know exactly how much they will be paying before signing a lease.
- Unmanageable fees can push tenants beyond the 30% income-to-housing cost threshold, leading to rental debt and eviction. These new protections will ensure the rent on your lease is the rent tenants pay every month.
- These new protections for tenants and accountability for landlords are needed to prevent predatory practices that allowed landlords to collect applications fees to no ends for tenants trying to find a place to call home
