Legislation
Governor Lombardo vetoed legislation
• Offering one-year rent stabilization for seniors
• That reformed rental application fees, increased security deposit transparency, and
required rental agreements to include a grace period for late rent payments
• That would halt evictions for up to 60 days for renters who have pending
applications for rental assistance
• Allow local incorporated governments to pass ordinances that would limit the
amount that landlords can demand for the leasing of a home or renewing a lease
• That would change Nevada’s summary eviction process, requiring the landlord to
initiate the eviction process with the court
Evictions In Nevada
Nevada has a rapid summary eviction
process
• In most states, the landlord must file an
eviction lawsuit with the court
• In Nevada, if a tenant receives a 7-day
eviction notice, the tenant must file an
aﬀidavit with the court
• If they do not, the tenant is evicted
The eviction process in Nevada places extraordinary burdens on the tenant
and favors the landlords

What is a Tenant Union?
What is Tenant Union?
Tenant unions are renters in a
building or neighborhood that
organize to fight for collective
interests and rights related to
housing
---
Brief History of Tenant Unions
1880’s Tenant activism began with the rise of industrialism
1920’s Tenant organization led to rent control in New York
1930’s During the great depression tenant organizations would block evictions through large protests
1960’s The Harlem rent strikes inspired tenant organization across the country
1969    The national tenant organization formed
1970’s Tenant organization led to rent control in Boston
1975    Tenant leaders founded shelterforce magazine to increase tenant activism
1980’s The New Jersey Tenant Organization demanded landlords put smoke detectors in every
apartment
2000’s 2008 recession saw a revival in tenant union activism
2020 2020 pandemic was a solidification that tenant unions where here to stay
