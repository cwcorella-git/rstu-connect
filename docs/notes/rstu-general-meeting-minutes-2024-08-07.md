### Member organizing request — Sarah, Kasey, Dalton, Robert

Tenants seeking assistance organizing. Conditions reported:

- Outlets are 2-prong and not up to code. Power shuts off if the electric load exceeds
  some unknown threshold — tenants have to reset the breakers multiple times to get the
  power back on.
- Landlord uses pesticides and poison against pests even though tenants have pets.
  Tenants reached out to a professional exterminator, who advised strongly against
  poison.
- Food issues:
  - Mouse and cockroach infestation — over 30 mice in one apartment, mice eating food.
  - Refrigerator has not been working for a month — food spoilage.
- Enters units without 24-hour notice.
- No repairs for tenants — dropped off refrigerator door handle parts and expected the
  tenant to fix it themselves.
- Maintenance is not completed timely.
- Landlord has not followed up on lease renewals.
- Only given one air filter per year for the furnace.
- Wants to know their rights — the landlord might sell the place or raise rents.
- Live on Ralston near the university; the landlord owns most of the buildings on the
  street.
- David provided advice:
  - Offered to provide links to evidence / laws / information upon request.
  - Nevada Legal Services is a great resource — information available online.
  - Civil Self-Help Law Center on the first floor of the Justice Court downtown, open
    9 AM–5 PM.
- RSTU Canvassing Committee would like to meet and start organizing in that
  neighborhood. Nix will share contact information.
- Building composition: 4x 1BD and 3x 2BD units — each tenant pays a different rent
  ($1,020, $1,005, $950, $1,050, etc.).
- The landlord used to be the accountant for the LLC owning the properties; their
  sibling, the previous landlord, passed away and the new landlord assumed the role.
- Meeting set for next Wednesday at 6:00 PM.

### TUF — Tenant Union Federation

- National union of tenant unions.
- Three or four key folks there formerly with People's Action.
  - KC Tenants is involved; the Connecticut union too.
- Provides training, support, capacity-building, membership models.
- Ultimately RSTU should get involved — early starters of RSTU were leaning on these
  people anyway.
- Shared a Tenants Bill of Rights.
- Ben suggests that 2 or 3 RSTU folks meet with them.

### RealPage algorithmic price fixing

- "RealPage" has created an algorithm for price fixing units.
- Shares tenants' private info about credit, lease, price they pay, etc.
- The algorithm suggests rent.
- They strongarm landlords into setting the suggested rents.
- The Arizona Attorney General launched a lawsuit to sue them.
  - There has been a movement to ask other AGs to investigate in their respective states.
- RealPage is having a convention at the Wynn in Vegas next week.
  - Groups are looking to disrupt.
  - Ben has drafted a letter and is asking RSTU to cosign in Discord.
