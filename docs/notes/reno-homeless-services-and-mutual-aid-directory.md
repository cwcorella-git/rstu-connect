A working referral list of free meals, shelter, and mutual aid in Reno, compiled in
January 2026 from a local unhoused-advocacy organizer's referral notes. Useful as a
quick handoff sheet when canvassing or when someone at a building needs food or shelter
tonight.

**Verify before referring.** Hours, sites, and eligibility change; the warming-center
site in particular rotates. Nothing below has been re-confirmed since January 2026.

## Organizations

- **Reno Unhoused** — <https://linktr.ee/renounhoused>
- **Spotlight Action — Hampton House** — <https://spotlightaction.org/hampton-house>
  Food pantry + community garden.
- **RISE / Reno Initiative** — <https://renoinitiative.org/>
- **The Radical Cat** — <https://theradicalcat.square.site/> (sign up for the newsletter)

## For those who are hungry

**Two free meals, Monday–Friday:**

- **Trinity Episcopal Church** — morning. Free bag lunches + coffee + dog and cat food.
  No ID required, everyone welcome. 9:30–10:30 AM.
- **St. Vincent's Dining Room** — free hot lunch daily, 11:30 AM–1:00 PM. Serves over
  1,400 people daily, 7 days per week.

**One free meal every Tuesday:**

- **Family Soup at Believe Plaza** — every Tuesday at 5:00 PM.

**Mutual Aid Mondays:**

- **Our Center** — every Monday at 6:00 PM.

**Free pet food, Monday–Friday:**

- **Trinity Episcopal Church** — morning, dogs and cats.

## For those who need shelter

- **Good Neighbors Warming Centers** (RISE + Hampton Garden) — 7 days per week,
  8:30 PM–6:00 AM. Site rotates between St. Thomas Aquinas, First United Methodist,
  Trinity Episcopal, and Good Shepherd Lutheran (Clay Street).
  Accepts women, women with children, or men with children. **Single men should be
  directed to CARES.**
- **CARES Campus** — 7 days per week, all welcome.
- **Crossroads** — transitional housing.
- **Our Place** — women's domestic violence shelter (often waitlisted).
- **Noah's Animal Shelter** — boards and feeds pets for people entering domestic
  violence shelters, where pets are not allowed.
