This farm would never have been a reality without all of our international and local supporters, including co-founder Tami Brunk, International Coordinator Kaya Michener, PDC Coordinator Wael Al Saad, Albert Bates and the Global Village Institute, Permaculture Designer Julie Firth, Alex Cicelsky at Kibbitz Lotan, the UK Permaculture Research Institite of Australia, Starhawk, Firedoll, Council of Arab Australian Relationship, Australia Aid and too many others to count. If you would like to support the Marda Permaculture Farm with a donation or other support, please let us know.
