## Te Haymarket Martyrs

## Lucy E. Parsons

## November 1926

Does his rising generaion know ha hose who inauguraed he eigh-hour day were pu o deah a he command o capial?

Unil ory years ago men, women and children oiled en and ofen welve hours a day in acories or a mere pitance, and children rom six o nine years o age had o work o help keep up he amily.

Te Knighs o Labor, a powerul organizaion claiming 500,000 members, had never agiaed or a reducion o he hours o labor. Ten who were he pioneers o he eigh-hour movemen? Tose maryrs who were srung rom he gallows in Chicago on November 11, 1887, he muchlied-abou and abused Anarchiss.

I will veriy his saemen. Unil 1885 here had never been a concered acion or he reducion o he hours o labor. I eigh hours was menioned in some o our meeings (hey were never really menioned), why, ha was only a dream o be indulged in by ools; he bosses would never olerae such a hing, was he reply.

In 1885 a convenion was held in Chicago, composed largely o delegaes rom Canada. Tey passed a resoluion calling upon he workers o his counry and Canada o unie in a demand or a reducion o he hours o oil o eigh a day on he firs o May, 1886, and o srike wherever i was reused.

Alber R. Parsons brough he mater up beore he rade and Labor Assembly o Chicago, he firs cenral labor body ever organized in his ciy, a body which he himsel organized and o which he was eleced presiden hree consecuive imes. Te mater was holy debaed and finally rejeced on he ground ha he bosses would never olerae i.

Te Cenral Labor Union, composed o German mechanics, ook he mater up and endorsed i. A he same ime hey passed a resoluion requesing Augus Spies, edior o he Chicago Arbeiterzeitung, he daily German paper, and Alber R. Parsons, edior o he Alarm, o suppor i in heir papers and speeches; hey were boh splendid oraors.

Tus i was ha he eigh-hour movemen go under way. Many oher ciies agiaed or i, bu Chicago was he sorm cener o he movemen, owing o he zeal and courage o he men and women o his ciy who worked day and nigh or i. Te resul was ha when May 1s, 1886, arrived, i ound Chicago well-organized and demanding he eigh-hour day, sriking by he housands where he demand was reused. I was a veriable holiday or he workers.

Te bosses were aken compleely by surprise. Some were righened and hreaening; some were signing up; ohers were abusing hose "scoundrels" who had brough all his rouble upon "our" ciy, and declaring ha hey would be made examples o, ha hey ough o be hung and he like.

Te police were unspeakably brual, clubbing and shooing; acory whisles blew, bu ew responded.

I was chairman o he Women's Organizaion Commitee and know personally how ha grea srike spread. I have never seen such solidariy.

Res, comrades, res. All he omorrows are yours!
