## **Queer Fire: The George Jackson Brigade, Men Against Sexism, and Gay Struggle Against Prison**

## **Date:** Unknown

**Source:** https://libcom.org/article/queer-fire-george-jackson-brigade-men-against-sexism-and-gay-struggle-against-prison

**Tags:** sex and sexuality, United States, LGBTQ+ (Lesbian Gay Bi Trans Queer +), 1970s, prisons, Washington, sexual violence, George Jackson Brigade, Carl Harp, Ed Mead

A collection of histories, speeches, and interviews with members of The George Jackson Brigade and Men Against Sexism. These stories give inspiration for the multiform queer struggle against prison, capitalism, and the state. This zine was compiled by Untorelli Press, andan audio version is available from Resonance Distro.
